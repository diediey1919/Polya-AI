# Semantic Usability & Rigor Audit
## Polya Apologetica Knowledge Base

---

### 1. Semantic Architecture & Standards
Every completed analysis file adheres to a strict multi-section epistemic schema:
- **PURPOSE:** Definitive research objective and scope.
- **INPUT:** Primary textual sources (Hebrew, Greek, Arabic, Latin, Syriac) + manuscript evidence.
- **CONDITION:** Contextual credibility, dating controls, and language separation.
- **KNOWLEDGE:** 20 to 50 chronological layers, distinction matrices, and critical apparatuses.
- **ACTION:** 30 Claim Tests (graded `SAFE`, `SAFE WITH QUALIFICATION`, `DISPUTED`, `NOT ESTABLISHED`, `UNSAFE`).
- **OUTPUT:** Error Register (E1–E20), 5 Global Steelmen, 15 Case Studies, Comparative Matrix.
- **DEPENDENCY:** Explicit prior STEP links (`STEP 61-62`, `STEP 64-100`, etc.).
- **NEXT STATE:** Explicit immutability handoff (`STEP N-1 UNCHANGED`, `STEP N+1 NOT STARTED`).

### 2. Semantic Integrity Findings
- **No Logical Gaps:** Logic, philosophy, and exegesis are distinguished from theological dogma.
- **Steelman Enforcement:** Christian, Islamic, Jewish, and Skeptical positions are steelmanned before critique.
- **No Manufactured Consensus:** Genuine historical and theological uncertainties are explicitly labeled `UNKNOWN` or `DISPUTED`.
- **Anti-Overclaim Invariant:** Linguistic stability is never equated with theological inspiration, and textual variants are never equated with malicious corruption.
