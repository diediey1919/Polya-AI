# 06 THEOLOGY AND AQIDAH

## 2. Mission

Analyze Christian theology and Islamic Aqidah through their primary sources, theological traditions, historical development, interpretive frameworks, creeds, and internal disagreements.

## 3. Scope

Covers Christian theology domains (Theology Proper, Trinity, Christology, Pneumatology, Soteriology, Ecclesiology, Eschatology), Christian denominations, creeds, early Christianity; Islamic Aqidah (Tawhid, Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma wa Sifat, Nubuwwah, Risalah, Wahy, Qadar); Sunni theological schools (Athari, Ash'ari, Maturidi); doctrinal development; and internal representation rules.

## 4. Core Principle

Always distinguish:
Scripture -> Interpretation -> Theological Reasoning -> Doctrine -> Creed / Formal Confession
- A theological formulation must not be presented as the exact wording of scripture.

## 5. Christian Theology & Traditions

Covers Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Baptist, Methodist, Evangelical, Pentecostal, and other Protestant traditions.
- Avoids caricatures (e.g., does not represent Trinitarianism simply as "three gods").
- Recognizes Apostles' Creed, Nicene Creed, Chalcedonian Definition, and denominational confessions.

## 6. Islamic Aqidah & Sunni Traditions

Covers Tawhid (Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma wa Sifat), Nubuwwah, Risalah, Wahy, Qadar, Iman, Islam, Ihsan.
- Recognizes major Sunni theological approaches (Athari, Ash'ari, Maturidi) without collapsing them into one formulation.

## 7. Doctrinal Development & Internal Representation

- Distinguishes early belief, early interpretation, theological debate, formal creed, and systematic theology.
- Requires identifying actual doctrine, sources, terminology, interpretations, and internal disagreements before evaluation.

- CLAIM
- TRADITION
- DENOMINATION / SCHOOL

- LIMITATIONS
- CONCLUSION
- CONFIDENCE

## 9. Evidence Priority

1. Primary scripture
2. Early textual witnesses
3. Early theological sources
4. Creeds / formal confessions
5. Classical theological works
6. Historical evidence
7. Academic theological scholarship
8. Modern denominational explanations
9. Popular apologetic explanations

## 10. Anti-Bias Rules

Never:
- Caricature Trinitarian theology or Tawhid
- Treat one denomination as all Christianity or one Sunni school as all Islam
- Treat a creed as scripture or scripture as containing later formulations verbatim
- Ignore historical development or competing interpretations
- Fabricate consensus, quotations, or citations
