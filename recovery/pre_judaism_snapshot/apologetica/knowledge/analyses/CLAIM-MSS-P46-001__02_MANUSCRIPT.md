# Analysis 02_MANUSCRIPT: P46 Textual Significance
P46 is one of the earliest substantial manuscript witnesses to the Pauline corpus, providing paleographic and textual-criticism data for early Christian manuscript culture. The codex is dated c. 175–225 CE and contains surviving portions of multiple Pauline epistles. Its textual character generally aligns with the Alexandrian text-type, with some Western readings in certain passages (e.g., Romans).

## Limitations
* P46 is not an autograph.
* Surviving content is incomplete.
* Original codex contents are uncertain.
* Hebrews authorship debate is not resolved by P46.
