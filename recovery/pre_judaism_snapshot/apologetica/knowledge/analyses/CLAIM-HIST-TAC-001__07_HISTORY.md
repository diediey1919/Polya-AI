# Analysis 07_HISTORY: Tacitus Christian Presence in Rome
*Annals* 15.44 is a key piece of external Roman historiography documenting the existence of Christians in Rome by the mid-1st century CE. Tacitus represents a non-Christian Roman senatorial voice, and his testimony complements the literary evidence of Christian sources (e.g., Paul, Acts) and the Jewish historiography of Josephus, while remaining independent of Christian and patristic tradition.
