C. GHADIR KHUMM EVENT & WORDING
==================================================

- Event: Public gathering at Ghadir Khumm during the return from the Farewell Pilgrimage (10 AH).
- Wording: "من كنت مولاه فعليّ مولاه" (Whoever I am his mawla, Ali is his mawla).
- Lexical Audit: "Mawla" encompasses master, patron, friend, helper, and protector. Multiple meanings do not inherently prove or disprove political succession without contextual and theological interpretation.
- Isnad & Collections: Wide transmission across early Sunni (Ahmad b. Hanbal, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Hakim) and Shi'i compendia, though common-link analysis (e.g., through Ibn 'Abbas, Zayd b. Arqam, Sa'd b. Abi Waqqas) points to robust early attestation.

==================================================
D. HADITH AL-THAQALAYN
==================================================

- Core Wording: "إني تارك فيكم الثقلين: كتاب الله وعترتي أهل بيتي" (I am leaving among you two heavy things: the Book of God and my progeny, my Ahl al-Bayt) vs. variants featuring "كتاب الله وسنتي" (Book of God and my Sunnah).
- Sahih Muslim Version: Preserves the major version emphasizing the Qur'an and Ahl al-Bayt through Zayd b. Arqam.
- Authority & Escalation: Establishes high reverence, obligation to love, and spiritual guidance of Ahl al-Bayt, but requires additional premises to establish infallibility, exclusive authority, or twelve-member succession.

==================================================
E. HADITH AL-MANZILAH
==================================================

- Core Wording: "أنت مني بمنزلة هارون من موسى إلا أنه لا نبي بعدي" (You are to me in the position of Aaron to Moses, except that there is no prophet after me).
- Context: Tabuk expedition where Ali was left in charge of Medina.
- Analogy & Limitation: Establishes exceptional closeness, deputyship, and high authority during the Prophet's absence, but because Aaron died before Moses, the analogy requires supplementary premises to prove post-prophetic political succession.
