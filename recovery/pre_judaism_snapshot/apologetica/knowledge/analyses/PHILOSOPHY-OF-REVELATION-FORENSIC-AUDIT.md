## 1. CORE DISTINCTIONS

To prevent category errors and philosophical conflation, the following conceptual boundaries are maintained strictly:

- **REVELATION (*Apokalupsis / Wahy*):** The disclosure of transcendent truths, divine will, or reality to human consciousness by divine initiative.
- **DIVINE REVELATION:** Ontological communication originating from an existent transcendent Creator.
- **GENERAL REVELATION:** Disclosure of divine attributes through nature, cosmos, and human conscience (Romans 1:20).
- **SPECIAL REVELATION:** Specific historical disclosures (theophany, incarnation, scripture, prophetic messaging).
- **PROPHETIC REVELATION:** Communication mediated through designated human messengers.
- **SCRIPTURAL REVELATION:** Canonical written texts embodying or recording revelation.
- **INSPIRATION:** The divine prompting or guidance of human authors/speakers ensuring truth.
- **ILLUMINATION:** The divine enablement of human minds to comprehend revealed truth.
- **VISION / DREAM / MYSTICAL EXPERIENCE:** Phenomenological inner experiences of transcendent realities.
- **MIRACLE / PROPHECY / TESTIMONY:** Empirical and historical corroborative warrants.
- **INTERPRETATION / DOCTRINE / TRADITION:** Human hermeneutical, systematic, and historical formulations derived from or built upon foundational texts.

---

## 2. CENTRAL PHILOSOPHICAL QUESTIONS

1. **Metaphysical Possibility:** Is divine revelation metaphysically possible? (Yes, if a personal, omnipotent Creator exists and possesses intentional agency).
2. **Transcendent-Finite Communication:** Can an infinite God communicate propositional truths to finite minds without distortion?
3. **Private vs. Public Revelation:** Can private mystical experiences serve as public truth warrants?
4. **Preservation:** Can propositional revelation be preserved across centuries through human manuscript transmission?
5. **Epistemic Adjudication:** When two mutually exclusive revelation claims (Christianity and Islam) both assert divine origin, what neutral criteria adjudicate between them?

---

## 3. METAPHYSICS OF REVELATION

- **Divine Agency & Transcendence:** If God is transcendent, communication requires divine condescension (*tanzil* / incarnation) bridging the ontological gap.
- **Causational Reality:** Distinguishing between natural cognitive processes and supernatural intervention requires rigorous evidential criteria to avoid collapsing revelation into psychological projection.

---

## 4. EPISTEMOLOGY OF REVELATION

Analyzed across classical and contemporary frameworks:
- **Foundationalism:** Requires self-evident epistemic baselines. Scriptural axioms risk circularity.
- **Reformed Epistemology (Plantinga):** Belief in God and divine revelation can be "properly basic" (warranted without discursive proof through the *Sensus Divinitatis* or Holy Spirit testimony). *Weakness:* Fails to adjudicate between mutually contradictory basic beliefs in competing religions.
- **Bayesian Epistemology:** Evaluates prior probabilities and cumulative testimonial evidence.

---

## 5. TESTIMONY AND REVELATION

- **Testimonial Epistemology:** Evaluates first-hand vs. second-hand manuscript testimony.
- **Epistemic Distinctions:** Sincerity does not guarantee cognitive accuracy; historical accuracy does not guarantee divine ontological origin; divine origin does not guarantee human hermeneutical infallibility.

---

## 6. LANGUAGE AND REVELATION

- **Finite Language vs. Infinite Reality:** Explored through cataphatic (affirmative), apophatic (negative), analogical (Aquinas), and metaphoric models.
- **Translation & Polysemy:** Recognizes that linguistic translation alters semantic horizons, necessitating rigorous original-language exegesis.

---

## 7. CHRISTIAN PHILOSOPHY OF REVELATION

- **Key Figures:** Augustine (divine illumination), Aquinas (grace builds on nature; natural theology + revealed mysteries), Calvin (internal testimony of the Holy Spirit), Barth (revelation as divine encounter breaking through human religion).
- **Plurality:** Spanning Catholic magisterial authority, Orthodox patristic consensus, and Protestant scriptural foundationalism (*sola scriptura*).

---

## 8. ISLAMIC PHILOSOPHY OF REVELATION

- **Key Concepts:** *Wahy* (inspiration/revelation), *Tanzil* (downward sending), *Nubuwwah* (prophethood), *Risalah* (messengership).
- **Philosophical Schools:** Athari (literal textualism), Ash'ari/Maturidi (rational theology balancing divine will and human acquisition), Mu'tazili (rationalist ethics and created Qur'an), Avicenna/Al-Farabi (emanationist epistemology of prophetic imagination), Al-Ghazali & Ibn Taymiyyah (critique of Hellenistic philosophy in favor of direct scriptural epistemology).

---

## 9. DIVINE HIDDENNESS

- **The Problem:** If God desires all to know and worship Him, why is divine revelation ambiguous, debated, and historically contested?
- **Responses:** Epistemic distance (John Hick), soul-making theodicy, and the preservation of authentic human free will.

---

## 10. RELIGIOUS DIVERSITY & ADJUDICATION

- **The Epistemic Dilemma:** Multiple global traditions claim divine revelation.
- **Adjudication Criteria:** Historical proximity, textual integrity, miracle attestation, internal coherence, moral adequacy, and explanatory power.

---

## 11. CIRCULARITY AUDIT

Analyzed propositions:
1. "The Bible is God's word because the Bible says so." (*Viciously circular unless grounded in independent historical validation*).
2. "The Qur'an is God's word because the Qur'an says so." (*Identical internal circular structure*).
3. "Jesus is trustworthy because Scripture says so." (*Dependent on manuscript transmission and historical reliability*).
4. "Muhammad is trustworthy because the Qur'an says so." (*Dependent on early historical attestation*).
