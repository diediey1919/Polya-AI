# Analysis 07_HISTORY: Irenaeus Historical Witness

## Evaluation
Irenaeus (c. 130–202 CE), Bishop of Lyons, wrote *Adversus Haereses* around 180 CE to refute Gnostic systems. In doing so, he explicitly defends the fourfold Gospel canon and cites the Gospel of John extensively, including Prologue terminology.

## Chronological Value
* **Composition Date:** c. 180 CE.
* **Significance:** Provides a firm terminus ad quem for the widespread ecclesiastical acceptance and use of the Gospel of John, predating the physical production date of major surviving uncial codices (Sinaiticus, Vaticanus) by over a century.

## Methodological Limitations
* Patristic attestation is literary/historical evidence of canon and reception, distinct from primary Greek manuscript textual transmission.
* Relies on medieval Latin manuscript transmission of Irenaeus's own work and occasional Greek fragments.
