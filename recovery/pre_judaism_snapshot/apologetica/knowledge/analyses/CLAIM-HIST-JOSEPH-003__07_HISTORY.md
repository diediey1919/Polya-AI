# Analysis 07_HISTORY: Testimonium Flavianum Authenticity (JOSEPHUS-TF-001)
The *Testimonium Flavianum* (Antiquities XVIII.3.3) exhibits complex transmission. While present in all surviving Greek manuscripts, its glowing confessional assertions regarding Jesus's messiahship, resurrection, and divine status have led critical scholarship to debate whether it is fully interpolated, partially authentic with Christian glosses, or originally a neutral/critical reference modified by later scribes.
