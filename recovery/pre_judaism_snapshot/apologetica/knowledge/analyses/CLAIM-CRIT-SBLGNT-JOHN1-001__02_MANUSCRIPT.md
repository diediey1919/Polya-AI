* **QUESTION:** What is the physical and textual independence status of SBLGNT relative to manuscripts and reference websites?
* **EVIDENCE_USED:** SBLGNT editorial preface and comparative textual analysis of John 1:1.
* **ANALYSIS:** 
  1. **Physical Source Independence**: **NO**. SBLGNT is a digital critical edition, not a physical historical manuscript. It is an editorial reconstruction derived from evaluating primary witnesses like Codex Sinaiticus (SRC-MSS-002) and Codex Vaticanus.
  2. **Editorial / Textual Independence**: **PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED**. While SBLGNT was newly edited by Michael W. Holmes based on an exhaustive comparison of Westcott-Hort, Robinson-Pierpont, Tischendorf, and modern critical editions (NA/UBS), its resulting text for John 1:1 is identical to standard modern critical editions (NA28/UBS5).
  3. **Comparison with Codex Sinaiticus**: **AGREEMENT**. SBLGNT's reconstructed text for John 1:1 matches the uncial witness of Codex Sinaiticus (*En archē ēn ho Logos...*).
  4. **Comparison with BibleHub & STEP Bible**: **AGREEMENT**. Reference websites display critical texts that align identically in wording with SBLGNT for John 1:1. However, the websites are mere digital presentation interfaces, whereas SBLGNT is a rigorously documented critical text edition.
* **COUNTERARGUMENT:** Critical editions are editorial constructs reflecting prevailing scholarly consensus rather than independent historical artifacts.
* **LIMITATIONS:** Editorial decisions involve calculated weighing of variant evidence; minor orthographic conventions (e.g., lower-case *logos*) differ from ancient uncial manuscripts.
* **CONFIDENCE:** STRONG EVIDENCE
