## A. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Forensic cross-system audit of hadith authentication methodologies across (1) Sunni, (2) Twelver Imami, (3) Zaydi, (4) Ismaili, and (5) modern academic historical-critical approaches. ## C. CRITICAL DISTINCTIONS (ENFORCED VOCABULARY)

HADITH ≠ QUR'AN (different transmission density, preservation mode, authority function). COLLECTION DATE ≠ REPORT ORIGIN DATE (Bukhari d. 256/870 compiles reports CLAIMING 2nd-century origins — two dates, two questions). ISNAD ≠ AUTOMATIC AUTHENTICITY (a chain is a CLAIM of transmission, not transmission itself). SAHIH ≠ AUTOMATIC MODERN HISTORICAL CERTAINTY (traditional grade answers "transmission-sound by our rules," not "event-certain by historiography"). WEAK ≠ AUTOMATICALLY FALSE (a *da'if* report may preserve true content via unsound route; weakness is route-verdict, not content-verdict). MULTIPLE CHAINS ≠ AUTOMATICALLY INDEPENDENT (see R/I0–I4). MULTIPLE BOOKS ≠ AUTOMATICALLY MULTIPLE WITNESSES (AB). COMMON LINK ≠ AUTOMATICALLY INVENTOR (Q). EARLY MANUSCRIPT ≠ AUTOMATICALLY AUTHENTIC CONTENT (manuscript dates COPYING, not composition — X). CANONICAL COLLECTION ≠ EVERY REPORT AUTHENTIC (F/H/I). SECTARIAN AFFILIATION ≠ AUTOMATIC FABRICATION; NON-SECTARIAN ≠ AUTOMATIC RELIABILITY (O). TRADITIONAL AUTHENTICATION ≠ HISTORICAL AUTHENTICATION; HISTORICAL PROBABILITY ≠ THEOLOGICAL AUTHORITY (AE/Z).

## E. SUNNI AUTHENTICATION (CRITERIA + HISTORICITY OF CRITERIA)

**E1. Figures/formations.** al-Zuhri (d. 124/742; court-scholar transmission hub; *tadwin*-commission reports) [L3–L4]; Malik (d. 179/795; *Muwatta'* — practice-anchored selection) [L6]; Musannaf literature ('Abd al-Razzaq d. 211/827; Ibn Abi Shaybah d. 235/847 — topical reservoirs preserving early material WITH mixed grades) [L6]; Musnad Ahmad (d. 241/855; ~27–30k reports, companion-arranged, UNGRADED reservoir: contains sahih→da'if by design) [L6–L7]; Bukhari/Muslim (F/G) [L7]; Four Sunan (Abu Dawud d. 275/889; Tirmidhi d. 279/892 — *'ilal*-sensitive grading vocabulary; Nasa'i d. 303/915; Ibn Majah d. 273/887 — weakest-canonical-status Sunan) [L7]; later critics (Daraqutni d. 385/995 *Ilzamat* vs Sahihayn; Ibn al-Salah L12 systematizer; Dhahabi/Mizzi rijal encyclopedias; Ibn Hajar *Taqrib/Tahdhib* + *Fath al-Bari*; Albani L14-contemporary re-grading movement) [L12–L14].

**E2. Nine criteria (mature Sunni formulation, L12; applied retroactively with care).** (1) **Isnad** (chain as transmission-claim). (2) **Matn** (text scrutinized for anomaly). (3) **Rijal** (narrator biographical science). (4) **Jarh wa ta'dil** (impugnment vs validation verdicts with STRICT precedence rules: explained jarh overrides vague ta'dil; *jarh mufassar muqaddam*). (5) **Ittisal** (continuity: no missing links; *'an'anah* scrutiny — contemporaneity + meeting-possibility, stricter in Muslim/Bukhari variants). (6) **'Adalah** (moral uprightness: Muslim, adult, sane, non-fasiq — sectarian innovator handled per O). (7) **Dabt** (precision: *dabt al-sadr* memory vs *dabt al-kitab* written; *saduq yahim* gradations). (8) **Shudhudh** (anomaly: trustworthy narrator contradicting stronger corroboration). (9) **'Illah** (hidden defect: subtle discontinuity/misattribution detectable only by masters — Tirmidhi/Daraqutni specialty). Sahih (mature def.): *ittisal + 'adalah + dabt + no shudhudh + no 'illah*.

**E3. Non-static control (AL-D/E).** L2–L3: Companion/Successor acceptance ran on trust + practice (*'amal*) + Companion-status, NOT five-condition machinery. L6: Malik screens by Medinan practice; Shafi'i (d. 204/820, *Risalah*) theorizes *khabar al-wahid* + *ahad*-conditions — first usul-formalization. L7: Bukhari/Muslim OPERATIONALIZE strictness (F/G) without writing the L12 manual. L12: Ibn al-Salah CANONIZES the five-condition definition pedagogically. Reading L12 back into L6/L7 as "always already there" is UNSAFE; continuity-with-formalization is SAFE.

## F. SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (COMPILATION → RECEPTION, LAYER-SEPARATED)

**F1. Compilation [L7].** Muhammad b. Isma'il al-Bukhari (d. 256/870): 16-year collection/travel; ~600,000 reports heard → ~7,563 entries (~2,600 unique sans repetition) selected. Criteria (reconstructed from practice + *Tarikh* + pupil reports): strict contemporaneity-plus-contact (*liqa'*) for *mu'an'an*; narrator *'adalah+dabt* at hadith-master standard; freedom from *'illah/shudhudh* via *mutaba'at/shawahid* cross-check; chapter-heading (*tarjamah*) jurisprudence guiding inclusion. NOT a theory-manual — method is EXEMPLIFIED, not codified (L12 codifies later).
**F2. Selection vs claim.** "Bukhari graded X sahih" = route-verdict by his rules. It does NOT equal "every historical detail in X independently established" (AL-AB): e.g., chronology details, numbers, companion-attribution framings inside an otherwise-sound report remain separately testable (S/R). Conflating grade with event-certainty is E3.
**F3. Transmission [L7–L13].** Pupil-recensions (Firabri most-circulated; Nasafi; Hammad); no autograph survives (X-control: earliest Sahihayn MSS are 4th–5th/10th–11th c. copies, not authorial holographs). *Fath al-Bari* (Ibn Hajar d. 852/1449) = post-classical canonization crown [L13].
**F4. Reception/authority [L12–L14].** *Asahh al-kutub ba'd Kitab Allah* status: post-hoc consensus (*talaqqi bi-l-qabul* = community reception as EPISTEMIC UPGRADE in Sunni usul — U). Daraqutni's *Ilzamat* (critique of ~200 Sahihayn chains) PROVES authority was argued, not automatic; mainstream Sunni answer: objections answered chain-by-chain (Nawawi/Ibn Hajar), reception-upgrade retained. Authority DEVELOPED (AL-G/H).

## P. ISNAD-CUM-MATN (ICMA — WHAT IT CAN/CANNOT DO)

Method (Motzki-school mature form) [L14]: collect ALL chains + wordings of one tradition; tabulate variants against transmitters; locate common links/partial common links; map geography/chronology; reconstruct earliest recoverable wording + its probable milieu. CAN: (i) falsify single-origin legends (divergent cores); (ii) date redaction-layers RELATIVELY (which wording presupposes which); (iii) identify regional traditions (Kufan vs Medinan profiles); (iv) corroborate early attestation when geographically-separated lines converge pre-130 AH. CANNOT: (i) deliver certainty about PROPHETIC origin (converges on earliest RECOVERABLE layer, usually Successor-generation — Motzki's own limit); (ii) self-validate without external anchors (coins, papyri, non-Muslim sources, archaeology — cf. STEP 101–104 material-evidence controls); (iii) escape the analyst's own dating-assumptions (rijal-dates reused = partial circularity, AA-screened). AL-R verdict: "can improve analysis" SAFE WITH QUALIFICATION.

## Q. COMMON LINK THEORY (NO AUTOMATIC INVENTOR)

Juynboll's thesis: the transmitter where multiple chains CONVERGE backward (common link, usually Successor-generation) is the likely ORIGINATOR (fabricator or first-formulator) [L14]. Audit: (i) single strand (*fard/gharib*) below the link is COMPATIBLE with genuine narrow transmission (early Islam's teaching-circles were small — Schacht's "argument from silence" overreaches); (ii) partial common links (two-student convergence) suggest teaching-circle reality, not forgery-committee; (iii) family isnads (*riwayat al-abna' 'an al-aba'*) show household-transmission plausibility; (iv) collector-transmission (Bukhari gathering Kufan lines) EXPLAINS convergence as COLLECTION-ARTIFACT (AB/AA-circularity screen); (v) diving BELOW the link via matn-variants sometimes recovers earlier wording (P-iv), contradicting inventor-thesis. Alternative explanations (genuine narrow transmission + collection-artifact + teaching-circle) must be EXCLUDED before inventor-verdict — AL-U SAFE (E8 refuted).

## R. MULTIPLE CHAINS (INDEPENDENCE LEVELS I0–I4)

**R1. Six confound-tests** per multi-chain claim: (1) independent witnesses? (2) shared source behind branches? (3) common transmitter (Q)? (4) common regional tradition (Kufan pool)? (5) literary borrowing (one compiler copying another's chain — AB)? (6) later harmonization (wordings aligned editorially — W)? **R2. Scale:** I0 = no independence (same source/chain recycled); I1 = unknown (insufficient data — DEFAULT for single-book multi-chain displays); I2 = partial (branches share early link, diverge later); I3 = probable (geographically-separated lines, no literary link, compatible variants); I4 = strong (I3 + external anchor: papyrus/coin/non-Muslim attestation or cross-sectarian convergence per AC). DEFAULT RULE: displayed multiplicity starts at I1 until tests passed — AL-S/T enforced (E6/E7 refuted).

## S. MATN CRITICISM (EIGHT LENSES — EVIDENTIAL, NOT AUTOMATIC)

(2) Anachronism (institutions/coins/titles postdating claimed setting — strongest academic tool; requires SECURE external dating). (3) Theological development (fully-formed kalam formulae inside Prophetic mouths = lateness-signal, graded not decisive). (4) Vocabulary (*gharib*-density, Persian/Greek loan-profile vs Hijazi baseline). (5) Historical context (plausibility against L1–L3 controls from sirah-material audits). (6) Legal development (report mirroring LATER madhhab-finalized positions suspiciously exactly = madhhab-backprojection screen). (7) Qur'anic consistency (T). (8) Competing versions (variant-stemmatics — W). CONTROL (AL-Q): matn-lenses are PROBABILISTIC; late attestation ALONE never rejects (E-control: "appears late" ≠ "is late" — L4–L5 written reservoirs + L13 commentary-preservation routinely surface early material late).

## T. HADITH AND QUR'AN (DIRECTIONAL DISCIPLINE)

Chain: Qur'an → Hadith → Tafsir → Fiqh → Aqidah (authority-flow). Rules: (i) NO hadith proves a Qur'anic meaning before its OWN authentication + interpretive-context check (asbab-context, variant-readings, companion-tafsir layering — cf. STEP 107–111 Qur'anic audits in store); (ii) Qur'an-certain (*qat'i*) constrains hadith-probable (*zanni*) in Sunni usul (majority: *khabar wahid* does not override qat'i-Qur'an; Hanafi *'umum*-caution vs Shafi'i inclusion — madhhab-variance is NORMAL, V); (iii) Imami: Imam-anchored hadith can SPECIFY Qur'an with authority Sunni usul denies isolated chains; (iv) academic: Qur'an is the FIXED POINT (L1-text stability per manuscript audits) against which hadith-float is measured — direction NEVER reversed without argument. Circularity screen (AA): tafsir-report → fiqh-ruling → aqidah-proof → back to validate the tafsir-report is a closed loop, not corroboration.

## U. HADITH AND AQIDAH (GRADE-OF-REPORT vs GRADE-OF-DOCTRINE)

Question: can ONE isolated report (*khabar wahid*) establish doctrine? SUNNI USUL: majority (Ash'ari/Maturidi/Athari-leaning kalam; Hanafi caution strongest) — *wahid* yields *zann* (probable knowledge) → suffices for PRACTICE, does NOT independently establish *'aqidah-qat'iyyah*; doctrine requires *mutawatir*/corroborated-mass-transmission or Qur'an-certain. Minority (some Athari literalists; modern Salafi-expansionists): authentic *wahid* in creed-matters OBLIGATES belief. IMAMI USUL: Akhbari past (trust Four-Books-mass) vs Usuli present (Bahbahani victory): single *sahih*-graded-by-rijal report CAN ground doctrine under Usuli ijtihad, *tawatur* ideal but not absolute gate; Imam-anchored chains upgrade epistemic weight. ZAYDI: near-Sunni caution + present-Imam/scholar confirmation. ISMAILI: Imam/*da'i*-authorization replaces report-counting (M2). DISTINCTION (AL — AF-matrix "Theological Dependence"): single-report / mutawatir / multi-independent / established-doctrine are FOUR different epistemic states — collapsing any two manufactures false certainty or false skepticism.

## V. HADITH AND FIQH (DISAGREEMENT ≠ REJECTION-OF-SUNNAH)

Legal-hadith machinery: apparent contradiction → *jam'* (reconciliation) → *tarjih* (preponderance) → *naskh*-claim (abrogation, heavily policed: requires chronology-proof) → *takhsis* (specification) → *siyaq*-context (occasion-restriction). Madhhab differences over one hadith ROUTINELY reflect: different grading (Hanafi *mursal*-rejection vs Maliki/Shafi'i-conditional-acceptance), different *'umum/takhsis* moves, different practice-anchors (Maliki *'amal* vs Iraqi *ra'y*-screening), NOT "rejecting Sunnah" (AL — E-control: intra-Sunnah disagreement is methodology-divergence). Case-pattern: raising-hands (*raf' al-yadayn*), recitation-behind-imam, *qunut* — all sides cite graded reports; verdicts diverge on *tarjih*-axioms, proving AUTHENTIC-SAME-POOL + DIFFERENT-WEIGHING is the normal state.

## W. HADITH VARIANTS (FIVE ETIOLOGIES — INFER WITH EVIDENCE)

Wording-additions/omissions/paraphrase/narrator-substitutions/chain-variants/chapter-placement. Candidate causes: (a) oral-transmission performance (*riwayah bi-l-ma'na* licit-paraphrase doctrine — Muslim's variant-preservation PROVES early scholars distinguished lafz/ma'na); (b) editorial arrangement (Bukhari's *taqti'* — cutting one hadith across chapters by legal point); (c) scribal transmission (copyist-error profiles in MSS — X); (d) multiple source traditions (genuine parallel hearings); (e) later development (harmonization/expansion). RULE (AL-V): variant-pattern must be MAPPED (P-stemmatics) before etiology-assigned — "variants therefore fabrication" and "variants therefore richness" are BOTH unevidenced. ADDITIONS (*ziyadat al-thiqah*: trustworthy-narrator additions) are the classic test-case: majority-acceptance with corroboration-checks vs strict-rejection — same data, different risk-axioms.

## X. HADITH MANUSCRIPTS (COPY-DATES, NOT COMPOSITION-DATES)

Hierarchy: autograph (NONE surviving for major collections — stated, not lamented) → authorial copy → student copy (recension-anchors: Firabri-Bukhari; Ibn Sufyan-Muslim) → early MSS (4th–6th/10th–12th c. fragments: e.g., Sahihayn early copies; Zahiriyah/Damascus holdings; Sana'a-adjacent finds for OTHER genres) → later MSS → printed editions (Bulaq/Cairo standardizations introduce EDITORIAL layer — L13-print). What MSS CAN test: textual stability BETWEEN copy-generations (collation deltas), recension-identities (which pupil-line a copy follows), interpolation-zones (marginalia-migration). What they CANNOT: authenticate CONTENT (E17 refuted — AL-W: manuscript age dates the COPY; a 5th-century copy of a forged report is a faithfully-copied forgery). Collection-specific honesty: Muwatta' (multiple recensions — Yahya al-Laythi vs others — recension-comparison possible); Musnad Ahmad (late stabilization; son-'Abd Allah additions MARKED — transparency-record); Kafi (Usuli-Akhbari-era MSS; *Mir'at*-era collation); *Da'a'im* (Fatimid-chancery MSS claims need codicological audit, marked UNVERIFIED where absent).

## Y. COLLECTION VS REPORT (SEVEN-FIELD SEPARATION, PER COLLECTION)

Template applied across F/G/H/I/L/M holdings: (1) COLLECTION DATE (author death + compilation window). (2) AUTHOR (biography + school-function). (3) SURVIVING MANUSCRIPTS (recension-lines + earliest dated copy or UNKNOWN). (4) TRANSMISSION HISTORY (pupil-lines → commentary-canonization). (5) REPORT DATE (claimed origin-generation per chain: L1/L2/L3). (6) EARLIEST ATTESTATION (first INDEPENDENT witness outside the collection's own lines — Q/P-tested). (7) LATER RECEPTION (grading-afterlife: *talaqqi*, re-gradings, polemical reuse). AL-X enforced: a collection is a HETEROGENEOUS CONTAINER (Musnad Ahmad's ungraded-reservoir design; Tusi's conflict-juxtaposition design) — never "one homogeneous witness." Double-counting (AB) and circularity (AA) screens run on fields (4)+(6), not on prestige.

## AA. CIRCULARITY (THREE LOOPS DETECTED + SCREENS)

**Loop-1 (narrator↔collection):** collection X authenticates narrator N (by including him) → N's presence authenticates report R in X → R proves X reliable. SCREEN: narrator-verdicts must cite PRE- or EXTRA-collection evidence (earlier rijal, independent lines, teacher-pupil corroboration outside X). **Loop-2 (tafsir→fiqh→aqidah):** see T-iv. SCREEN: directional-discipline + independent Qur'an-anchor. **Loop-3 (academic rijal-recycling):** common-link dating USES rijal death-dates whose reliability derives from the same tradition under test. SCREEN: mark recycled-date conclusions I2-maximum; demand external anchors (papyri/coins/non-Muslim witnesses) for I3+. All circular structures must be NAMED before the argument using them is admitted.

## AB. DOUBLE COUNTING (BOTH DIRECTIONS POLICED)

Sunni-side: one report in Bukhari + Muslim + Tirmidhi + Ibn Majah = ONE transmission-line family REUSED by four compilers (shared teachers: e.g., Qutaybah/Humaydi circulation), NOT four witnesses — default I1→I2 until R-tests pass (AL-T; E7 refuted). Imami-side: Kafi → Tusi (Tahdhib/Istibsar) → Saduq (Faqih) chains LARGELY REPRODUCE the same Kufan *usul*-lines — three books citing one stream = ONE stream with three taps (AL; E-control). Academic-side: five articles citing the SAME Juynboll-line are not five confirmations. RULE: count TRANSMISSION-STREAMS (source-critical), not BOOK-TITLES (bibliographic).

## AC. CROSS-SYSTEM OVERLAP (SHARED MATERIAL, SIX-FIELD PROTOCOL)

Per shared report: EARLIEST ATTESTATION / SHARED WORDING / VARIANT WORDING / TRANSMISSION PATH / INDEPENDENCE (I-scale) / THEOLOGICAL RECEPTION. Sample-classes (representative, not exhaustive): (i) Thaqalayn-formulations (Sunni + Twelver + Zaydi attestation — cf. STEP 125 in-store; wording DIVERGES on *'itrah*-scope → I2–I3 with redaction-questions open); (ii) Ghadir Khumm core-event (broad attestation; *mawla*-meaning contested → event-I3, interpretation-system-dependent); (iii) Alid-virtue reports via shared Kufan narrators (Sunni *fada'il* + Imami *manaqib* — same transmitters, opposed framing → independence LOW, motive-analysis HIGH); (iv) legal-practice reports (wudu/salah-formulae across Sunni+Zaydi — practice-convergence corroborates independently of chains). AL-AA verdict: cross-sectarian agreement is EVIDENTIALLY USEFUL (independent-motive convergence) but NEVER automatic independence (E20 refuted — shared Kufan pool explains much overlap at I1–I2).

## AD. CONFLICTING AUTHENTICATION (8-STEP PROTOCOL, NO DEFAULT-TRIBALISM)

When Sunni grade ≠ Imami grade: (1) SUNNI REASON (which conditions fail/pass, which rijal verdicts cited). (2) IMAMI REASON (rijal-grid + Imam-anchor/taqiyyah role). (3) NARRATOR EVIDENCE (biographical data both sides accept vs dispute). (4) TEXTUAL EVIDENCE (variant-map: do wordings even match? W/P). (5) CHRONOLOGY (layer-tags: are both grading the SAME historical claim? D). (6) INDEPENDENT HISTORICAL EVIDENCE (external anchors). (7) LOGICAL ASSUMPTIONS (name each side's axioms — authority, taqiyyah, bid'ah-rules). (8) SUNNI THEOLOGICAL EVALUATION (AG-formula: accepted/rejected/preferred + methodological basis — WITHOUT claiming "Sunni grade = historical proof"). Worked mini-application: Kufan Alid-virtue report graded *hasan-li-ghayrihi* (Sunni: corroborated Kufan lines) vs *sahih* (Imami: Imam-anchored + loyal narrators) — steps 1–7 CONVERGE on "same stream, different axioms"; step 8 records Sunni practice-upgrade WITHOUT event-certainty inflation.

## AE. HISTORICAL PROBABILITY (H0–H6 — FIREWALLED FROM GRADES)

H0 impossible/contradicted (external refutation) / H1 extremely weak / H2 weak / H3 plausible / H4 probable / H5 strongly supported / H6 very strongly supported (convergent independent streams + external anchor). FIREWALL (AL-Y): H-scale NEVER translates 1:1 from sahih/hasan/muwaththaq/da'if — a *sahih*-by-grade report with single narrow stream and no anchor sits at H2–H3 (probable-transmission, plausible-event); a *da'if*-by-grade report with cross-sectarian I3 convergence can reach H4 (weak-route, probable-content). Grade answers ROUTE; H answers EVENT. Confusing them is E19/E3.

## AG. POLYA SUNNI EVALUATION (POST-RECONSTRUCTION VERDICTS)

"SUNNI THEOLOGICAL EVALUATION:" — (1) Authentic Sunnah (Sahihayn-anchored, *talaqqi*-received) is AUTHORITATIVE for belief and practice; *wahid*-in-creed handled by majority caution (U). (2) Twelver/Zaydi/Ismaili methods REJECTED as authority-systems (Imam-axiom, taqiyyah-hermeneutic, ta'lim-authorization lack Qur'an-Sunnah-Sahabah warrant) but their TRANSMISSION-DATA (shared narrators, early Kufan lines, variant wordings) remains usable historical evidence under Sunni-critical screening. (3) Academic ICMA ACCEPTED as auxiliary dating/corroboration tool, REJECTED where its skepticism-axioms (e.g., Schachtian wholesale-backprojection) prejudge outcomes. (4) Grade ≠ historical proof: Sunni theology claims AUTHORITY for authentic Sunnah, not historiographic certainty for every detail (F2). Basis: Qur'an (obedience-texts), mutawatir Sunnah-practice, Sahabah-transmission (*'adalah al-sahabah*), early Sunni scholarship (Shafi'i→Bukhari→Ibn al-Salah line).

## AH. SUNNI STEELMAN (STRONGEST FORM)

Isnad-criticism with 1400-year biographical infrastructure (Mizzi/Dhahabi/Ibn Hajar scale unmatched in pre-modern historiography); continuity-arithmetic (liqa'-rules) as forgery-friction; *'illah*-detection (Daraqutni) catching errors modern stemmatics rediscovers; mutaba'ah/shawahid as native corroboration-logic; *tawatur*-theory formalizing mass-transmission epistemology (mathematically serious: independent-convergence argument); established-practice (*'amal*) anchoring text to lived community; self-critical record (Daraqutni's *Ilzamat* PRESERVED inside the tradition, not suppressed).

## AI. SHI'I STEELMANS (THREE, SEPARATE)

**TWELVER:** Imam-anchored transmission (if Imams are authoritative, chains through them are super-chains — axiom-dispute, not method-failure); rijal-bibliography (Najashi's author-control targets EXACTLY the forgery-vector academics allege); *muwaththaq*-licence proving non-tribal reliability-judgment; Majlisi's report-by-report grading proving self-critical capacity; *taqiyyah*-hermeneutic as genuine conflict-resolution logic given persecution-premise. **ZAYDI:** broad-tent corroboration (uses Sunni + Alid streams, maximizing independent lines); present-scholar verification (living accountability vs closed-canon fideism); Mu'tazili-rationalist screening of anthropomorphic/transmission-absurd reports (matn-criticism cognate). **ISMAILI:** authorization-epistemology (if a living authoritative teacher exists, transmission-gaps are BRIDGED not fatal — coherent given the premise); *ta'wil*-layering preserving meaning-stability across wording-variance; Fatimid-chancery preservation (state-archival vector).

## AJ. ACADEMIC STEELMAN (STRONGEST FORM)

ICMA variant-mapping (P) exposing redaction-layers traditional grading never sees; common-link geography (Q, screened) dating regional traditions; external-anchor discipline (coins/papyri/non-Muslim sources) breaking Loop-3 (AA); Schacht's legal-development chronology (where textually controlled) explaining madhhab-backprojection (S-6); Motzki's restraint (Successor-layer honesty) modeling how to claim LESS and prove MORE; manuscript-collation (X) testing stability empirically. Strongest when it DATES LAYERS, weakest when it LEAPS from "undatable" to "fabricated."
