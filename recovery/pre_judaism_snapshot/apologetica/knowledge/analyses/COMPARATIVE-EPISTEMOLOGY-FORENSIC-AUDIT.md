## 1. CENTRAL QUESTION

**Investigate:** "How can a rational person determine whether Christianity, Islam, both, or neither provides the most justified account of religious truth?"

- **Distinctions Maintained:** Truth, belief, justification, knowledge, certainty, probability, coherence, reliability, evidence, testimony, revelation, faith, and reason are kept strictly distinct to prevent categorical category errors.
- **Methodological Neutrality:** No religious system is assumed to be true at the starting gate. Symmetrical evidentiary and logical controls are applied universally.

## 2. EPISTEMOLOGICAL FRAMEWORKS

Evaluated across nine core models:
1. **Foundationalism:** Basic foundational beliefs supporting superstructure. *Strength:* Logical structure. *Weakness:* Arbitrary foundation choice.
2. **Coherentism:** Beliefs justified by mutual coherence. *Strength:* Avoids infinite regress. *Weakness:* Circularity and isolation from external reality.
3. **Reliabilism:** Beliefs justified by reliable cognitive processes. *Strength:* Externalist grounding. *Weakness:* Circularity in verifying reliability.
4. **Evidentialism:** Beliefs justified by proportional public evidence. *Strength:* Public accountability. *Weakness:* Restrictive criteria for historical anomalies.
5. **Testimonial Epistemology:** Knowledge transmitted via testimony. *Strength:* Essential for human learning. *Weakness:* Susceptible to legend and bias.
6. **Bayesian Epistemology:** Prior probabilities updated via likelihood ratios. *Strength:* Mathematical rigor. *Weakness:* Subjective prior probability assignment.
7. **Reformed Epistemology (Plantinga):** Properly basic beliefs (*sensus divinitatis* / *fitrah*). *Strength:* Captures doxastic practice. *Weakness:* Fails to adjudicate between mutually exclusive basic beliefs.
8. **Cumulative Case Reasoning:** Convergence of independent probabilistic lines. *Strength:* Explanatory power. *Weakness:* Subjectivity in probability aggregation.
9. **Virtue Epistemology:** Focus on epistemic character traits. *Strength:* Ethical grounding. *Weakness:* Underdetermines truth of specific supernatural claims.

## 3. REASON AND FAITH

- **Harmonization:** Neither religion is caricatured as blind fideism or pure rationalism.
- **Christian Tradition:** Faith seeking understanding (*fides quaerens intellectum*), Thomistic natural theology paired with revealed dogmas, and Calvinist internal testimony.
- **Islamic Tradition:** Integration of *aql* (reason) and *naql* (transmitted revelation), *fitrah* (innate disposition), and *dalil* (rational demonstration).

## 6. TESTIMONY & TEXTUAL EPISTEMOLOGY

- **Testimonial Rules:** Sincerity does not guarantee accuracy; accuracy does not guarantee divine ontological origin; divine origin does not guarantee human hermeneutical infallibility.
- **Textual Controls:** Distinguishing manuscript preservation, textual authenticity, hermeneutical meaning, and divine inspiration. Symmetrical standards applied to biblical papyri and qur'anic manuscript traditions (*San'a*, *Uthmanic* fragments).

---

## 7. MIRACLES, PROPHECY & HISTORY

- **Miracles:** Evaluated under Bayesian prior probabilities and Humean scrutiny. Guarding against reciprocal circularity (miracle proves prophet, prophet proves miracle).
- **Prophecy:** Tested for prior dating, textual stability, specificity, and independence from post-event editorial retrodiction.
- **History:** Evaluated using source proximity, multiple independent attestation, and criterion of embarrassment.
