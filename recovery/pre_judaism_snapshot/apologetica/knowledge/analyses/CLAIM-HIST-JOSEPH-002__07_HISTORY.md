# Analysis 07_HISTORY: James Passage Evaluation (JOSEPHUS-JAMES-001)
*Antiquities* XX.9.1 mentions James as the brother of "Jesus who was called Christ". Critical scholarship generally regards this passage as possessing high historical plausibility and lower interpolation likelihood than the Testimonium, providing external attestation of James and early Christian leadership in Jerusalem.
