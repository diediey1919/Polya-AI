# Analysis 07_HISTORY: Christus under Pontius Pilate
Tacitus's reference to the execution of "Christus" under Pontius Pilate during Tiberius' reign provides independent non-Christian Roman confirmation of the basic historical framework of Christ's execution under Roman authority, a detail also referenced in Josephus and in the New Testament creeds.
