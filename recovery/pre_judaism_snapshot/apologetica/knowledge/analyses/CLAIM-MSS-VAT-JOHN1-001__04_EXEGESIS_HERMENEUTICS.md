## 1. Textual Data
Codex Vaticanus attests the Greek text: EN ARCHE HN HO LOGOS KAI HO LOGOS HN PROS TON THEON KAI THEOS EN HO LOGOS (John 1:1).

## 2. Hermeneutical Framework
The primary observation is that no substantive textual variation undermines the core Johannine prologue as preserved in an independent Alexandrian witness (Codex Vaticanus).

## 3. Exegetical Note
From the standpoint of textual verification, the foundational word-play and semantic richness of the prologue (Word = λόγος, relating to God) are carried without interruption in this manuscript. The 4th-century attestation stabilizes the text against later misreadings or expansions.
