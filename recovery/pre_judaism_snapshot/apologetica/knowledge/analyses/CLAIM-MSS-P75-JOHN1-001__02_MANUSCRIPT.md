# Specialist Analysis: 02_MANUSCRIPT (CLAIM-MSS-P75-JOHN1-001__02_MANUSCRIPT)

## Paleographic & Textual Evaluation
Papyrus 75 ($\mathfrak{P}^{75}$) provides a crucial early benchmark for the text of John 1:1. Dated paleographically to c. 175–225 CE, its physical text precedes the 4th-century uncials (Sinaiticus and Vaticanus) by more than a century, while displaying a remarkably close textual affinity with Codex Vaticanus. 

## Independence & Transmission Value
While physically independent from Codex Vaticanus, Sinaiticus, and Papyrus 66 as an artifact, its textual relationship reflects early Alexandrian line transmission. It confirms that the standard Johannine prologue wording was firmly established in Egypt by the late 2nd or early 3rd century.
