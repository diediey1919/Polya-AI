# Analysis 07_HISTORY: Josephus Historical Framework
Josephus's works (*The Jewish War* [c. 75–79 CE] and *Antiquities* [c. 93–94 CE]) provide essential Greco-Roman and Jewish historical attestation for the geopolitical, social, and religious environment of first-century Palestine.
