D. KEY VERSE ANALYSES
==================================================

1. Q2:124 (Abrahamic covenant and Imam): Establishes divine designation of leadership (imam) for Abraham and righteous descendants, but does not explicitly name Ali or twelve successors without external hadith input.
2. Q4:59 (Ulil-amr): Commands obedience to God, Messenger, and those in authority among you (ulil-amr). Sunni tafsir identifies rulers/scholars; Shi'i tafsir identifies the infallible Imams. Requires hadith/theological premise to equate with Twelver infallibility.
3. Q5:55 (Wilayah verse): "Your wali is God, His Messenger, and the believers who establish prayer and give zakat while bowing." Historical reports link to Ali giving charity during ruku', but linguistic-contextual analysis shows 'wali' can denote helper/friend/master without inherently establishing political succession or divine designation.
4. Q5:67 & Ghadir: Proclamation verse analyzed alongside Ghadir traditions; textual connection depends on secondary exegetical frameworks.
5. Q33:33 (Purification verse): Establishes exceptional purity and honor for Ahl al-Bayt, but contextually addresses the Prophet's wives while hadith al-Kisa extends the designation to Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn. Does not explicitly establish political succession or infallibility.
6. Q42:23 (Mawadda fi'l-qurba): Establishes affection for kin/relatives as recompense for the prophetic message, interpreted variously across Sunni (love for relatives generally) and Shi'i (loyalty to Ahl al-Bayt) traditions.
