## 26. LOGICAL CONSISTENCY

Formalized: G = divine essence (one); F,S,H = hypostases (three); each fully divine (has the one essence); F≠S≠H by relation of origin. Contradiction requires same-predicate negation (P(x) ∧ ¬P(x)); essence-predicates and person-predicates differ, so 1≠3 arithmetic alone proves nothing (category-error flag). Surviving costs: (a) intelligibility of eternal relations; (b) simplicity vs real distinctions; (c) trilemma pressure modalism/tritheism/partialism — each classical model pays somewhere; costs graded DISPUTED–PROBABLE, never ESTABLISHED-refutation by logic alone.

## 27. TAWHID LOGICAL MODEL

Tawhid solves person-plurality by denying it and inherits attribute-plurality as its own cost — symmetry held.
