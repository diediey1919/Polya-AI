## 1. Textual Integrity
The manuscript preserves the linguistic structure that carries the philosophical content of John 1:1 underpinning later doctrinal formulations. As an attestation, Vaticanus does not introduce any new philosophical claims, nor does it obscure the logical framework of the statement.

## 2. Logical Flow
The articulated text in Vaticanus:
- Establishes a temporal premise ("In the beginning … was the Word").
- Introduces a relational proposition ("the Word was toward God").
- Concludes with a categorical statement ("God was the Word").
Preservations maintain the logical scaffolding without substantive alteration.

## 3. Metaphysics & Theology
This analysis records only the textual form. It does not adjudicate any metaphysical or theological conclusion; those belong to systems of belief that were constructed after the manuscript's preservation.
