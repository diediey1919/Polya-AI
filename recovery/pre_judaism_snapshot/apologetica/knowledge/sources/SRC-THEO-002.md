# SRC-THEO-002: Surviving Fragments and Letters of Arius

## Metadata
* **SOURCE_ID:** `SRC-THEO-002`
* **SOURCE_CATALOG_ID:** `THEO-ARI-FRAG`
* **Author:** Arius of Alexandria (c. 256–336 CE)
* **Source Type:** Primary Theological Source / Fragments (NOT a Biblical Manuscript or Autograph).

## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** Documented positions of Arius preserved in fragments (e.g., *Letter to Eusebius of Nicomedia*, *Letter to Alexander of Alexandria*, fragments of the *Thalia*), asserting that the Son was created/begotten *ex nihilo*, had a beginning ("there was when he was not"), and is subordinate in essence to the unbegotten Father.
* **Cannot Establish:** Complete unedited treatises (as most of Arius's writings were ordered burned by imperial edict), or unmediated access outside opponent transmission.
