# SRC-PAT-001: Irenaeus of Lyons - Adversus Haereses

## Metadata
* **SOURCE_ID:** `SRC-PAT-001`
* **SOURCE_CATALOG_ID:** `PAT-IRE-001`
* **Author:** Irenaeus of Lyons (c. 130–202 CE)
* **Work:** *Adversus Haereses* (*Against Heresies*), composed c. 180 CE.
* **Source Type:** Patristic Source / Historical Witness (NOT a Biblical Manuscript or Autograph).
* **Languages:** Greek (original composition), Latin (principal surviving translation for complete books).

## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** Historical attestation that Johannine literature and Logos theology (including citations corresponding to John 1:1) were actively known, cited, and regarded as authoritative in Christian theological discourse by c. 180 CE.
* **Cannot Establish:** Primary biblical manuscript readings, original New Testament autographs, direct divine inspiration, or theological truth by virtue of patristic quotation.
