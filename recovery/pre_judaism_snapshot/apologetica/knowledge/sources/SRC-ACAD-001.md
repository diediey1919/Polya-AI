# SADEGHI & GOUDARZI (2012)

**Full Title:** "Ṣanʿāʾ 1 and the Origins of the Qurʾān"
**Authors:** Behnam Sadeghi, Mohsen Goudarzi
**Journal:** Der Islam
**Volume:** 87(1–2)
**Pages:** 1–129
**Year:** 2012
**Type:** Peer-reviewed academic article

## Content

- Partial reconstruction of lower text of DAM 01-27.1
- Transcription of legible portions
- Variant classification and analysis
- Comparison with standard Uthmanic text
- Methodological framework for palimpsest analysis
- Historical conclusions about early Quranic transmission

## Methodology

- Digital image processing (post-processed by Alba Fedeli)
- UV imaging to reveal lower text
- Systematic transcription of visible traces
- Variant categorization (orthographic, lexical, morphological, etc.)
- Comparison with Cairo 1924 edition
- Analysis of surah ordering

## Key Findings

- Lower text contains significant variants from standard Quran
- Surah order differs from standard Quranic sequence
- Variants include word substitutions, omissions, additions, syntactic rearrangements
- Estimated 25× more variants than Ibn Mas'ud codex
- Lower text represents non-Uthmanic tradition

## Critical Notes

- Reconstruction based on visible traces, not complete text
- Some readings uncertain due to erasure and overwriting
- Lower text is fragmentary (not complete Quran)
- Dating assumptions based on radiocarbon and palaeography
