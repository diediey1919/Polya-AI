# SOURCE RECORD: SRC-MSS-002

* **SOURCE_ID:** SRC-MSS-002
* **SOURCE_NAME:** Codex Sinaiticus
* **REPOSITORY:** Codex Sinaiticus Project (British Library, Leipzig University Library, National Library of Russia, Saint Catherine's Monastery)
* **URL:** https://codexsinaiticus.org/
* **SOURCE_TYPE:** PRIMARY MANUSCRIPT / DIGITAL MANUSCRIPT REPOSITORY
* **MANUSCRIPT_ID:** Codex Sinaiticus (Gregory-Aland $\aleph$ [Aleph] or 01; von Soden $\delta$ 2)
* **DATE / PERIOD:** 4th Century CE (c. 330–360 CE)
* **LANGUAGE:** Ancient Greek (Koine / Biblical Greek)
* **SCRIPT:** Uncial (Majuscule) script, continuous script (*scriptio continua*) in four columns per page.
* **MATERIAL:** Prepared animal vellum (parchment), high-quality calfskin or sheepskin.
* **CONTENTS:** Septuagint (Old Testament) and complete New Testament, along with the Epistle of Barnabas and the Shepherd of Hermas.
* **CURRENT_LOCATION:** Distributed across four institutions: British Library (London), Leipzig University Library, National Library of Russia (St. Petersburg), and Saint Catherine's Monastery (Sinai).
* **PROVENANCE:** Copied in the 4th century (likely in Egypt or Caesarea), housed for centuries at Saint Catherine's Monastery, Sinai; acquired by the Russian Imperial Library in the 19th century, later purchased by the British Museum (British Library) in 1933, with portions discovered at Sinai in 1975.
* **CATALOG / SHELFMARK:** British Library Add. 43725, Leipzig Cod. Friderico-Augustanus, etc.
* **TARGET:** New Testament, Gospel of John, specifically John 1:1.

* **AUTHORITY_LEVEL:** A — PRIMARY / DIRECT EVIDENCE (per SOURCE_REGISTRY.md)

  - Paleographic analysis
