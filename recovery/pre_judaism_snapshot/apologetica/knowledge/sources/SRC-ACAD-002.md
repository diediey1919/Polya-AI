# HILALI (2017)

**Full Title:** The Sanaa Palimpsest: The Transmission of the Qur'an in the First Centuries AH
**Author:** Asma Hilali
**Publisher:** Oxford University Press / Institute of Ismaili Studies
**Series:** Qur'anic Studies Series
**Year:** 2017
**Type:** Academic monograph with annotated edition

## Content

- New annotated edition of both upper and lower texts
- Critical introduction with new hypotheses
- Contextualization within Quran manuscript studies
- Historical and institutional contexts of transmission
- Systematic reference to previous studies

## Key Findings

- 35 of 38 folios relevant for analysis
- 61 textual variants identified from Cairo edition in lower text
- Lower text dated to 7th century CE
- Upper text dated to 8th century CE
- Lower text may be product of 'reading circle'
- Both layers show "work in progress" characteristics

## Methodology

- Annotated edition format
- Comparison with Cairo 1924 edition
- Analysis of both upper and lower texts
- Attention to institutional and pedagogical contexts
- Cautious transcription approach (fewer variants than Sadeghi)

## Critical Notes

- More conservative variant count than Sadeghi & Goudarzi
- Emphasizes pedagogical/institutional context
- Suggests lower text may be educational exercise rather than canonical codex
- Both layers fragmented and incomplete
