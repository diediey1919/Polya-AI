# TÜBINGEN QURAN MANUSCRIPT

**Common Name:** Tübingen Quran / Ma VI 165
**Collection:** University Library, Tübingen
**Catalogue Identifier:** Ma VI 165
**Repository:** Eberhard Karls Universität Tübingen, Universitätsbibliothek
**Physical Location:** Tübingen, Germany
**Manuscript Type:** Partial codex
**Surviving Content:** Approximately 77 folios (partial Quran)
**Script Type:** Hijazi (with some Kufic features)
**Writing Material:** Parchment
**Dimensions:** Not specified in available sources

## Provenance
- Acquired by Johann Gottfried Wetzstein in 1864 in Damascus
- Part of the Wetzstein collection at Tübingen University Library
- Identified and radiocarbon-dated in 2012

## Radiocarbon Dating
- **Laboratory:** ETH Zürich (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology)
- **Sample:** Parchment
- **Calibrated Range:** 649-675 CE (95.4% confidence)
- **Date Type:** Parchment date (death of animal), NOT writing date
- **Critical Note:** Range overlaps with Uthmanic period (644-656 CE) and extends shortly after

## Palaeographic Dating
- **Script:** Hijazi with transitional features toward Kufic
- **Scholarly Date:** 8th century CE (Déroche)
- **Critical Note:** Palaeography suggests later date than radiocarbon range

## Textual Characteristics
- Conforms to Uthmanic rasm tradition
- Some orthographic variants
- Minimal diacritical marks
- Verse separators present

## Scholarly References
