# CODEX PARISINO-PETROPOLITANUS

**Common Name:** Codex Parisino-petropolitanus
**Collection:** Multiple repositories
**Catalogue Identifier:** BnF Arabe 328 (parts a-f); Marcel 18/1 (St. Petersburg); Vat. Ar. 1605/1 (Vatican); KFQ 60 (Khalili)
**Repository:** Bibliothèque nationale de France (primary); National Library of Russia; Vatican Library; Khalili Collections
**Physical Location:** Paris, France; St. Petersburg, Russia; Vatican City; London, UK
**Manuscript Type:** Partial codex (multiple parts from different manuscripts)
**Surviving Content:** Approximately 26% of Quran (parts a+b); additional parts (c-f) from other manuscripts
**Script Type:** Hijazi
**Writing Material:** Parchment

## Structure

### BnF Arabe 328(a)
- **Folios:** 56 leaves (foll. 1-56)
- **Additional Leaves:** Vatican (Vat. Ar. 1605/1), Khalili (KFQ 60), National Library of Russia
- **Content:** Approximately 26% of Quran
- **Script:** Hijazi

### BnF Arabe 328(b)
- **Folios:** 14 leaves (foll. 57-70)
- **Content:** Originally part of same manuscript as 328(a)

### BnF Arabe 328(c-f)
- **Note:** Different manuscripts bound together
- **328(c):** 16 leaves (foll. 71-86); Birmingham fragments (carbon-dated to before 645) formerly bound with this

## Provenance
- **Origin:** Amr ibn al-As mosque, Fustat (old Cairo), Egypt
- **Acquisition:** Late 18th - early 19th century by Jean-Joseph Marcel and Jean-Louis Asselin de Cherville
- **BnF:** 1864 (part); earlier acquisitions
- **St. Petersburg:** 1864 (Public Imperial Library, now National Library of Russia)

## Palaeographic Dating
- **Script:** Hijazi (angular, rightward slant)
- **Scholarly Date:** Late 7th - early 8th century CE (Déroche)
- **Alternative Dates:** Some scholars suggest mid-7th century; others suggest early 8th century
- **Critical Note:** Multiple scribal hands identified (5 copyists per Déroche)

## Textual Characteristics
- Conforms largely to standard Uthmanic rasm
- Orthographic variants present (many "mere orthographic" per Déroche)
- Some differences not explained by orthography alone
- Minimal diacritical marks
- No systematic vocalization

## Codicology
- **Scribal Hands:** 5 copyists identified (Déroche)
- **Ruling:** Present
- **Verse Markers:** Six oval dots in three pairs
- **Surah Divisions:** Blank spaces
- **Every Fifth Verse:** Red alif encircled by dots

## Scholarly References
- Déroche, François. "La transmission écrite du Coran dans les débuts de l'islam." (2009)
- Déroche, François. "Qur'ān of the Umayyads." (2014)
- Corpus Coranicum database

## Independence
- **Physical Independence:** YES (multiple repositories)
- **Textual Independence:** DISPUTED (likely descends from Uthmanic exemplar tradition)
- **Genealogical Relationship:** NOT ESTABLISHED
