# SAMARKAND KUFIC QURAN

**Common Name:** Samarkand Quran / Tashkent Quran / Uthman Quran / Samarkand codex
**Collection:** Hast Imam Library
**Catalogue Identifier:** Inventory No. 1
**Repository:** Hast Imam Library (Telyashayakh Mosque), Tashkent, Uzbekistan
**Physical Location:** Tashkent, Uzbekistan
**Manuscript Type:** Fragmentary codex
**Surviving Content:** Approximately one-third of Quran (353 folios; surahs 2:7 through 43:10, with gaps)
**Script Type:** Kufic
**Writing Material:** Parchment (thick)
**Dimensions:** 53 cm × 68 cm
**Folios:** 353 extant folios
**Lines per Page:** 8-12 lines

## Provenance
- **Traditional Attribution:** Uthman ibn Affan (d. 656 CE) — NOT ESTABLISHED
- **Legendary History:** Brought to Samarkand by Timur (1370-1405) as war trophy
- **1869:** Acquired by Russian general Abramov from Khodja-Ahrar Mosque, Samarkand
- **1869-1917:** Imperial Library, St. Petersburg
- **1917:** Returned to Ufa by Lenin
- **1924:** Returned to Tashkent
- **1989:** Transferred to Hast Imam Library

## Radiocarbon Dating
- **Laboratory:** Oxford
- **Sample:** Parchment folio
- **Calibrated Range:** 
  - 640-765 CE (68% probability)
  - 595-855 CE (95% probability)
  - Alternative: 775-995 CE (95.4% confidence, per Rezvan)
- **Date Type:** Parchment date (death of animal), NOT writing date
- **Critical Note:** Wide range reflects uncertainty; palaeography suggests 8th-9th century

## Palaeographic Dating
- **Script:** Kufic (angular, formal)
- **Scholarly Date:** 8th or 9th century CE
- **Déroche Assessment:** Second half of 8th century (under Abbasid caliph Al-Mahdi, 775-785 CE)
- **Jeffery Assessment:** Early 9th century
- **Shebunin Assessment:** First thorough description (1891)

## Textual Characteristics
- Conforms to Uthmanic rasm tradition
- No vocalization
- No diacritical marks (i'jam)
- Simple ornamental elements (diagonal line panels at verse endings)
- Colored medallions (blue, green, red, manganese) for every tenth verse

## Codicology
- **Script Features:** Open form of 'ayn, occasional confusion between kaf and ta
- **Elongation:** Extended horizontal strokes in alif and lam
- **Binding:** Not specified in available sources
- **Facsimile:** 1905 edition by S.I. Pisarev (50 copies; reliability challenged)

## Uthmanic Attribution
- **Traditional Claim:** This is one of Uthman's five original codices
- **Scholarly Consensus:** NOT one of Uthman's original codices
- **Reason:** Script, orthography, and dating all point to 8th-9th century
- **Multiple Manuscripts:** Several manuscripts claim to be "Uthman's Quran" — none verified
