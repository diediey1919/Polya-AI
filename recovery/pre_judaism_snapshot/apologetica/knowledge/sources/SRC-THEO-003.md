# SRC-THEO-003: The Niceno-Constantinopolitan Creed (381 CE Formulation)

## Metadata

* **Author / Council:** First Council of Constantinople (381 CE)
* **Source Type:** Conciliar Creed / Theological Source (NOT a Biblical Manuscript, Autograph, or Canon List).

## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** Fourth-century post-Nicene conciliar expansion affirming Nicene Christology (*homoousios* with the Father) while significantly expanding pneumatological clauses concerning the Holy Spirit (Lord, giver of life, proceeding from the Father, worshipped and glorified together with Father and Son).
* **Cannot Establish:** The original 325 Nicaea text without modifications, or the medieval Latin *filioque* addition in the original Greek formulation.
