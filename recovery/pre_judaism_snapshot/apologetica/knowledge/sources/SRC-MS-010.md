# TOPKAPI QURAN MANUSCRIPT

**Common Name:** Topkapi Quran / Topkapi manuscript
**Collection:** Topkapi Palace Museum Library
**Catalogue Identifier:** H.S. 32 (historically H.S. 22, H.S. 44/32)
**Repository:** Topkapı Sarayı Müzesi (Topkapi Palace Museum), Istanbul, Turkey
**Physical Location:** Istanbul, Turkey
**Manuscript Type:** Near-complete codex
**Surviving Content:** 97.78% of Quran (408 folios; only 2 folios / 23 verses missing)
**Script Type:** Kufic (with Hijazi elements per some scholars)
**Writing Material:** Parchment (vellum)
**Dimensions:** 41 cm × 46 cm; text area 32 cm × 40 cm; codex thickness 11 cm
**Folios:** 408 extant folios

## Provenance
- **Acquisition:** Sent to Ottoman Sultan Mahmud II by Mehmed Ali Pasha, Governor of Egypt, in 1226 AH / 1811 CE
- **Traditional Attribution:** Uthman ibn Affan (d. 656 CE) — NOT ESTABLISHED by material evidence
- **Blood Stain Tradition:** Claimed to show blood stains from Uthman's assassination — NOT VERIFIED

## Palaeographic Dating
- **Script:** Kufic (some scholars note Hijazi features in certain folios)
- **Scholarly Date:** Late 1st century / early 2nd century AH (early-mid 8th century CE)
- **Altıkulaç Assessment:** Second half of 1st century AH to first half of 2nd century AH
- **Déroche Assessment:** Umayyad period (661-750 CE) based on script, illumination, vowel marking
- **Halaseh 2024:** Folios 2r-6v and 11 appear to be later additions by different hand
- **Critical Note:** NOT from 7th century (i.e., not from Uthman's lifetime)

## Textual Characteristics
- Conforms to Uthmanic rasm
- Contains non-canonical textual variants (Halaseh 2024)
- Vowel notation using red dots (not consistent across folios)
- Illumination present (surah divisions, ornamental panels)
- Some scribal errors documented (Altıkulaç)

## Codicology
- **Illumination:** Geometric/scroll motifs in gold ink
- **Surah Separations:** Illuminated horizontal bands
- **Verse Markers:** Present
- **Chapter Numbers:** Absent (consistent with early manuscripts)
- **Multiple Hands:** Evidence of different scribal hands

## Uthmanic Attribution
- **Traditional Claim:** This is Uthman's personal mushaf
- **Scholarly Consensus:** NOT Uthman's personal manuscript
- **Reason:** Script, illumination, and dating all point to 8th century, not 7th century
- **Altıkulaç:** "Must have been copied from the Muṣḥafs attributed to Caliph ʿUthmān"
