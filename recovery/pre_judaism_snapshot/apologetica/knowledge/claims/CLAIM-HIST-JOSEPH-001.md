# CLAIM-HIST-JOSEPH-001: Josephus Historical Context
Flavius Josephus (c. 37–100 CE) provides first-century Jewish and Greco-Roman historical evidence regarding Palestinian socio-political structures, religious sects, Roman administration, and regional context in *The Jewish War* and *Antiquities of the Jews*.
