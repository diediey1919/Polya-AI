# CLAIM-HIST-JOSEPH-003: Testimonium Flavianum Status (JOSEPHUS-TF-001)
The passage known as the *Testimonium Flavianum* (Antiquities XVIII.3.3) exists in the transmitted Josephus manuscript tradition, but its authorial authenticity remains a disputed and contested scholarly issue, with multiple critical models ranging from complete interpolation to partial authenticity.
