# CLAIM-HIST-TAC-002: Christus-Pilate Connection
Tacitus's *Annals* 15.44 records that the figure called "Christus" was executed under Pontius Pilate during the reign of Tiberius, providing an external Roman historical reference to the execution of Christ/Christians.
