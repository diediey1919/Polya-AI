# CLAIM-MSS-P46-002: P46 Early Pauline Transmission
P46 provides early manuscript evidence (c. 175–225 CE) for the transmission of Pauline texts, demonstrating the existence of an early and substantial manuscript tradition for the Pauline corpus.
