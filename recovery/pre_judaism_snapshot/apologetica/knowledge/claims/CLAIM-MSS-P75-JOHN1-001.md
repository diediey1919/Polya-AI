# Claim: Papyrus 75 Witness to John 1:1 (CLAIM-MSS-P75-JOHN1-001)

## Statement
Papyrus 75 ($\mathfrak{P}^{75}$, Bodmer XIV-XV) provides an early 3rd-century physical manuscript witness to the text of John 1:1 (*Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος, καὶ ὁ λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν θεόν, καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος*), demonstrating early textual stability and close alignment with the Alexandrian textual tradition (particularly Codex Vaticanus).
