## 2. OUSIA & HYPOSTASIS AUDIT
* **Ousia (οὐσία):** Refers to the common divine essence, nature, or being shared equally and indivisibly by the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (*what* God is).
* **Hypostasis (ὑπόστασις):** Refers to the individual, concrete subsistence, or distinct personal reality of each member of the Trinity (*who* God is: Father, Son, Spirit).
* **Formula:** The Cappadocian synthesis crystallized the orthodox formula as **μία οὐσία, τρεῖς ὑποστάσεις** ("one essence, three hypostases"), distinguishing general nature from specific individual properties (such as unbegottenness, begottenness, and procession).

## 4. STEELMAN INTERPRETATIONS
* **Interpretation A (Clarification & Preservation):** Cappadocian terminology is a natural conceptual unfolding and linguistic clarification of biblical monotheism and apostolic witness, protecting the reality of Christ's full divinity and the Holy Spirit against subordinationist errors.
* **Interpretation B (Development & Refinement):** Cappadocian theology represents significant conceptual and philosophical development, employing middle-Platonist and Greco-Roman philosophical categories to systematize doctrines not explicitly formulated in exact 1st-century vocabulary.
