## 1. Phonology & Orthography
Codex Vaticanus exhibits standard 4th-century Koine uncial orthography. In John 1:1 the Greek words are fully articulated (no nomina sacra abbreviations visible), and the text adheres to standard paleographic conventions for the period.

## 2. Morphology
- **EN ARCHE** (`Ἐν ἀρχῇ`): Preposition + Genitive Noun (indefinite Article present but not written: ho + arche, -> en arche).
- **HN** (`ἦν`): Verb, imperfect (it was/was existing).
- **HO LOGOS** (`ὁ λόγος`): Nominative singular masculine Article + Noun (the Word).
- **KAI** (`καὶ`): Conjunction (the first of two paratactic clauses).
- **HN** (`ἦν`): Verb, imperfect (repeat of IHs to mark the second clause).
- **PROS TON THEON** (`πρὸς τὸν θεόν`): Preposition + Article + Noun (toward God).
- **THEOS EN HO LOGOS** (`θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος`): Nominative singular masculine Noun + Verb + Article + Noun (God was the Word).

## 3. Syntax
- **Nominal Predicate Structure:** The anarthrous predicate nominative structure (logos THEOS einai) is preserved intact.
- **Paratactic Clause:** Two consecutive imperfect verbs (hn/tris) coordinate independent clauses; the strict absence of a conjunction (kai) in the second clause is precisely as attested and carries no textual variance.
- **Prepositional Construction:** Prōs to theon signals relationship to God, without substantive modification.

## 4. Comparison Summary
Codex Vaticanus John 1:1 aligns with Codex Sinaiticus and critical editions (SBLGNT, NA28) identically at morphological and syntactic lines. No discernible differences at the level of Greek phonology, morphology, or syntax.
