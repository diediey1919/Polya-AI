## 1. Executive Summary

Forensic audit of alleged Bible–Qur'an contradictions, proposition-by-proposition. Core finding: most popular "contradictions" dissolve under the formal test (identical subject, predicate, time, context, sense, reference) into twelve distinct verdict classes — only a residue of genuine THEOLOGICAL DISAGREEMENT (e.g., crucifixion mechanics, divine sonship, Trinity) survives as irreducible, and even there the audit separates textual, historical, and theological layers rather than declaring blanket falsification in either direction. Difference is not contradiction; contradiction is not falsification. Both Muslim lists ("Bible contradicts Qur'an") and Christian lists ("Qur'an contradicts Bible") are judged by the identical battery.

## 2. Scope

Covers creation, Adam/Eve, Noah/flood, Abraham/sacrifice, Moses/Exodus, David, Solomon, Jesus (birth, Messiah, Son of God, divinity, prophethood), crucifixion (P1–P6), resurrection, Trinity, Muhammad/prophecy, preservation (bounded — full treatment STEP 137), chronology, translation, context, exegesis; 30 claim tests; 15 case studies; scoring framework (heuristic only); five steelmen; philosophical audit; error register; confidence matrix; limitations; handoffs; conclusions. No shallow popular-list compilation.

## 3. Methodology

Pipeline segregation (TEXT → TRANSMISSION → TRANSLATION → CONTEXT → INTERPRETATION → THEOLOGY → DOCTRINE → APOLOGETIC CLAIM); formal contradiction test P(x,t,c) vs NOT-P(x,t,c) with six identity conditions; source hierarchy (primary text → critical edition → manuscripts → early transmission → early interpretation → classical exegesis → scholarship → later theology → apologetics → popular internet, last never authoritative); original-languages-first rule (Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek/Arabic before translations); steelman-before-critique; symmetry across Muslim/Christian lists.

## 4. Contradiction Definition

Logical contradiction requires same subject, same predicate/claim, same time, same relevant context, same sense, same reference — such that both cannot be true together in the same context. Failed identity in any condition → reclassify (translation/linguistic/contextual/chronological/interpretive difference, etc.), never auto-label contradiction. Verdict classes (12): TRUE CONTRADICTION, APPARENT CONTRADICTION, TRANSLATION DIFFERENCE, LINGUISTIC DIFFERENCE, TEXTUAL VARIANT, INTERPRETIVE DIFFERENCE, CONTEXTUAL DIFFERENCE, CHRONOLOGICAL DIFFERENCE, THEOLOGICAL DISAGREEMENT, SOURCE/TRADITION DIFFERENCE, UNDERDETERMINED, UNSUPPORTED CLAIM. Multiple labels may co-apply.

## 5. Source Hierarchy

Applied per case: (1) primary text, (2) critical edition, (3) manuscript evidence, (4) early transmission, (5) early interpretation, (6) classical exegesis, (7) scholarly research, (8) later theology, (9) apologetic literature, (10) popular/internet claims. Popular websites never primary authority; where a case rests only on level-10 sources it is flagged UNSUPPORTED until anchored higher.

## 9. Creation

Duration ("six days" both corpora — yom vs yawm semantic ranges compared; no contradiction on bare duration), sequence (Genesis ordered days vs Qur'anic heaven/earth passages with varied literary order — literary arrangement ≠ chronological assertion; CONTEXTUAL/INTERPRETIVE DIFFERENCE), heavens-and-earth priority (Q 2:29 vs 79:27–30 orientations — classical tafsir itself debates sequence; UNDERDETERMINED at text level), angels/Iblis command (Qur'anic Iblis among commanded angels — jinn identity vs function-based inclusion; INTERPRETIVE DIFFERENCE). No TRUE CONTRADICTION established at text level; several APPARENT CONTRADICTIONS resolved by sense/context analysis.

## 10. Adam

## 11. Noah
