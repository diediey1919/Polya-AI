## 2. NICAEA 325 VS CONSTANTINOPLE 381 COMPARISON
* **Wording Retained:** Reaffirmed the core anti-Arian Christology of Nicaea 325 (true God of true God, begotten not made, *homoousios* with the Father, incarnation, crucifixion under Pontius Pilate, resurrection, ascension, and return).
* **Wording Modified / Trimmed:** Removed the specific 325 anathemas against Arius at the conclusion; streamlined phrasing regarding the divine persons.
* **Wording Expanded:** Substantially expanded the articles concerning the Holy Spirit (describing Him as Lord, the Giver of Life, proceeding from the Father, who with the Father and the Son is worshipped and glorified, who spoke through the prophets) and expanded the concluding articles on the Church, baptism, resurrection, and eternal life.

## 3. HOLY SPIRIT & FILIOQUE AUDIT
* **Original Greek Formulation:** States that the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father (*ek tou patros ekporeuomenon*).
* **Filioque Absence:** The original 381 Greek text does **not** contain the Western *filioque* ("and from the Son") clause, which was a later regional Latin addition introduced centuries later in the West.
* **Claim Test:**
  A. "The Niceno-Constantinopolitan Creed originally contained the filioque." — **FALSE / UNSUPPORTED**
  B. "The Western filioque formulation was already part of the 381 creed." — **FALSE / UNSUPPORTED**
  C. "The 381 creed speaks about the Spirit proceeding from the Father." — **SAFE**
