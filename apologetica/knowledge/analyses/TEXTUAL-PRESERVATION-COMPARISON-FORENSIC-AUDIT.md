## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Comparative preservation audit of three textual traditions under one methodology: Qur'an (Q), Hebrew Bible / Tanakh (HB), New Testament (NT). Central verdicts: (a) no tradition possesses its autographs; earliest recoverable text in each case is a reconstruction, not an excavated original; (b) Q shows the tightest consonantal-skeleton stability across early witnesses (Hijazi rasm coherence) with bounded, catalogued reading variation (qira'at) — verdict SUBSTANTIAL STABILITY, not verbatim-zero-variation; (c) HB shows deep plurality in antiquity (DSS: proto-MT, LXX-like, SP-like forms side by side) converging on a highly stable medieval MT — verdict EARLY PLURALITY → LATER STABILITY; (d) NT shows the richest and earliest manuscript attestation with substantial variation concentrated in a small minority of readings — verdict EARLY ATTESTATION + BOUNDED VARIATION. Global slogans ("Bible corrupted," "Qur'an perfectly unchanged") are UNSUPPORTED without operational definitions; per-witness, per-variant verdicts below. Dependency-linked (STEP 02, 101–112, 132); no new sources.

## 2. SCOPE

Traditions: Q, HB (incl. DSS, LXX, SP, Targums, MT), NT (papyri, uncials, minuscules, lectionaries, versions, Fathers). Dimensions per §34 list (38 sections). Excluded: full contradiction battery (STEP 136), canon-theology apologetics, miracle philosophy (STEP 141).

## 3. METHODOLOGY

Evidence-layer → witness-layer → variant-layer → reconstruction-layer → theological-layer; epistemic symmetry (§24/26); independence audit I0–I4; variant classes MINOR/MODERATE/SIGNIFICANT/MAJOR; preservation defined operationally (§22) before any verdict; paraphrase-and-flag where wording unverified.

## 4. TERMINOLOGY

AUTOGRAPH: author's own text/edition — CLAIM TYPE: HISTORICAL CLAIM; no autograph survives for any tradition (ESTABLISHED). EARLIEST RECOVERABLE TEXT: oldest text reconstructible from witnesses — an inference, not an artifact. EARLIEST MANUSCRIPT/WITNESS: oldest surviving physical carrier or citation. CRITICAL TEXT (NA28/UBS, BHS/BHQ, SBLGNT; Corpus Coranicum materials): editorial reconstruction — not an autograph. RASM: consonantal skeleton; QIRA'AH: canonical reading tradition; RIWAYAH/TARIQ: transmission line; AHRUF: early seven-forms concept, theories disputed (§14). CANON (book list) ≠ TEXTUAL UNIFORMITY. Variation ≠ corruption; stability ≠ revelation — both need argument.

## 6. MANUSCRIPT DATING

C14 dates writing material (parchment), not the ink event — a terminus post quem for the animal, not a copy date. Palaeography dates script style within ranges (decades, not years). Codicology/ink/context/attestation constrain further. MANUSCRIPT DATE ≠ COMPOSITION DATE in every tradition. Uncertain dates reported as ranges with method tags, symmetrically.

## 7. HEBREW BIBLE

Proto-MT tradition coexisted with non-MT Hebrew forms (DSS evidence); MT (medieval codices: Aleppo, Leningrad) is a superbly stable custodial text — minuscule consonantal agreement across a millennium of MT copying. LXX preserves distinct Hebrew Vorlagen in places; SP and Targums add further witnesses. Verdict: LATER STABILITY built atop EARLY PLURALITY (STRONG EVIDENCE).

## 8. DEAD SEA SCROLLS

Qumran biblical manuscripts (3rd c. BCE–1st c. CE): proto-MT, LXX-affine, SP-affine, and non-aligned texts side by side in one sectarian library. Verdicts: diversity ESTABLISHED; "proves corruption" UNSUPPORTED (plurality is a transmission fact, not a corruption verdict); "proves modern MT word-identical to all antiquity" UNSUPPORTED (MT matches one ancient stream closely, not every stream).

## 9. SEPTUAGINT

Greek translation (Torah 3rd c. BCE; rest over following centuries) from Hebrew Vorlagen partly divergent from proto-MT. Translation technique varies book to book (literal ↔ paraphrastic). Rule: distinguish Hebrew VARIANT from Greek TRANSLATION DIFFERENCE before any preservation claim. LXX is simultaneously a witness to lost Hebrew readings and a theological document in its own right.

## 10. NEW TESTAMENT

Papyri (P45, P46, P52, P66, P75, others), uncials (Sinaiticus, Vaticanus, Alexandrinus, Bezae), minuscules (thousands), lectionaries, versions (Latin, Syriac, Coptic), patristic citations. No two extensive manuscripts identical; the great majority of variation is orthographic/grammatical; the critical text (NA28/UBS) is stable across editions at the overwhelming majority of points. Earliest fragment (P52, conventionally early 2nd c., range disputed) ≠ whole-NT attestation.

## 11. NT VARIANTS

Classes: ORTHOGRAPHIC / GRAMMATICAL / WORD ORDER / LEXICAL / OMISSION / ADDITION / SUBSTITUTION / TRANSLATION / HARMONIZATION / SIGNIFICANT. Doctrine-bearing variants (a small set) each get individual verdicts — no global "no doctrine affected" assertion without per-variant evidence (§20), and no inflation of spelling counts into doctrinal differences (§25).

## 12. QUR'ANIC MANUSCRIPTS

Early Hijazi codices/fragments, parchment, papyri, inscriptions (epigraphy ≠ codex). Key witnesses: Birmingham parchment, Sana'a (incl. palimpsest), Parisino-Petropolitanus, Tübingen fragment, Topkapi, Samarkand (traditional attributions flagged, not assumed), Dome of the Rock inscriptions (epigraphic, non-codex witness). Rasm coherence across early Hijazi witnesses is high; diacritic/vocalization systems develop later. Traditional attribution ≠ demonstrated provenance — every Ottoman-era claim gets a provenance flag.

## 13. SANAA

Per STEP 103R: UPPER TEXT (Uthmanic-type rasm) and LOWER TEXT (earlier textual form, washed/erased, reconstructed with uncertainty) audited separately. Lower text differs in sequence, orthography, and some lexical choices; reconstruction carries dating, provenance, and legibility uncertainty. Verdicts: "proves corruption" UNSUPPORTED; "irrelevant" UNSUPPORTED; exact finding: bounded early textual plurality within a tradition that standardized on the Uthmanic rasm (PROBABLE).

## 14. RASM

Consonantal skeleton; early Hijazi orthography underspecified relative to later fully-pointed printing. RASM ≠ QIRA'AH ≠ AHRUF ≠ RIWAYAH (STEP 104). Rasm differences are textual facts, not automatic separate revelations; qira'at operate substantially within rasm compatibility (with catalogued exceptions debated).

## 15. QIRA'AT

Canonical readings with transmission isnads; QIRA'AH/RIWAYAH/TARIQ distinguished. Verdicts: "qira'at = different Qur'ans" UNSUPPORTED (shared skeleton, shared core text, one tradition's reading plurality); "qira'at = merely pronunciation" UNSUPPORTED (consonantal and lexical differences exist in the catalogued set — MINOR class, theologically bounded but textually real).

## 16. AHRUF

Seven-ahruf hadith evidence with multiple classical theories (dialects; reading types; content categories; early textual forms) and modern academic proposals. Relationship ahruf→qira'at→rasm: DISPUTED/UNCERTAIN. No single theory presented as established fact.

## 17. UTHMANIC STANDARDIZATION

Per STEP 106: Abu Bakr collection (Zayd, Hafsa suhuf reports) → Uthman committee → regional mushafs → rasm standardization; destruction-of-rivals reports are literary-historical claims requiring source criticism, not physical evidence. Verdicts: "standardization = creation from nothing" UNSUPPORTED; "standardization proves every regional difference vanished" UNSUPPORTED; "standardization alone proves modern-print identity" UNSUPPORTED — the inference needs manuscript corroboration, which substantially exists for rasm but with the Sana'a-type caveats.

## 18. CANON

Christian canons differ by communion (Protestant 66; Catholic +deuterocanonicals; Orthodox/Tewahedo/Syriac extensions) — BOOK-LIST facts, not textual-reading facts. Qur'anic mushaf contents stable across Sunni/Shi'i printed traditions (chapter-order/reading differences bounded; polemical inflation flagged). Never confuse canon difference with variant-reading difference.

## 19. CRITICAL EDITIONS

NA28/UBS (NT), BHS/BHQ (HB), SBLGNT, DSS editions, Corpus Coranicum resources: all are editorial reconstructions from evidence, versioned and revisable. Citing a critical text as "the original" is a category error; citing its apparatus as "corruption" is equally an error — the apparatus is the evidence of careful transmission science.

## 20. MANUSCRIPT INDEPENDENCE

Per-witness I0–I4 audit (physical/editorial/genealogical/textual). Shared rasm tradition (Qur'anic witnesses), shared textual ancestry (NT Alexandrian/Byzantine clusters; MT custodial stream), and shared exemplars all discount independence symmetrically. N websites imaging one manuscript = ONE witness.

## 21. VARIANT CLASSIFICATION

Every variant: TYPE / SIGNIFICANCE / TEXTUAL / TRANSLATIONAL / THEOLOGICAL / HISTORICAL impact; grades MINOR/MODERATE/SIGNIFICANT/MAJOR. Significant variant ≠ doctrinal contradiction — impact argued per variant, never by global slogan.

## 22. DOCTRINAL IMPACT

Trinity/Christology/resurrection/atonement (NT variants); tawhid/revelation/qira'at/legal verses (Qur'anic readings): each doctrine-bearing candidate evidenced individually. Blanket "no doctrine affected" claims rejected unless per-variant demonstrated; blanket "doctrine X refuted by variant Y" claims rejected unless the variant's text actually bears the doctrine.

## 23. TRANSLATION

Hebrew→Greek→Latin→vernaculars; Arabic→world languages: translation technique, Vorlage choice, and receptor-language limits create differences that are NOT textual variants. Every alleged contradiction routed through original-language check first (STEP 136 §25 dependency).

## 24. PRESERVATION DEFINITIONS

A verbatim identity; B substantial stability; C skeleton stability; D reading-tradition stability; E canonical-text stability; F semantic stability; G message stability. Findings: Q achieves C–E at STRONG level, A at DISPUTED (qira'at/Sana'a bounds); MT achieves B–E from medieval period onward at STRONG level with ancient-plurality caveat; NT achieves B/F/G at STRONG level with early-variation caveat. "Perfect preservation" without selecting A–G is equivocation (E10).

## 27. APOLOGETIC CLAIMS

"400,000 variants": inflated aggregate of mostly trivial differences across thousands of witnesses — MISLEADING WITHOUT CLASS BREAKDOWN. "Qur'an has no variants": UNSUPPORTED (qira'at + rasm history real). "Every Bible manuscript different": trivially true at orthographic level, MISLEADING at substantial level. "Every Qur'an manuscript identical": UNSUPPORTED (early orthographic + reading bounds). "Uthman burned all rivals": literary report, PHYSICAL EVIDENCE ABSENT. "Qira'at prove multiple Qur'ans" / "qira'at only pronunciation": both UNSUPPORTED (§15). "DSS prove unchanged / prove corrupted": both UNSUPPORTED (§8). "Sinaiticus proves modern identity": UNSUPPORTED (one 4th-c. witness ≠ modern print). "Sana'a proves corruption / proves zero variation": both UNSUPPORTED (§13). "Critical text = invented Bible": UNSUPPORTED (reconstruction from evidence). "Uthmanic rasm = no textual history": UNSUPPORTED.

## 28. QUANTITY VS QUALITY

Preservation judged on date, distribution, geography, diversity, independence, coverage, quality, provenance — never manuscript counts alone. NT's thousands do not auto-win; Qur'an's rasm coherence does not auto-prove verbatim identity; MT's millennium stability does not erase ancient plurality.

## 29. HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION

TEXTUAL reconstruction (likely earlier readings) distinguished from HISTORICAL reconstruction (events) and THEOLOGICAL reconstruction (doctrines). A reconstructed reading is a probability, not a revelation certificate and not a corruption certificate.
