## A. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Primary Object

The Ṣanʿāʾ Palimpsest (DAM 01-27.1, also designated Ṣanʿāʾ I or Sanaa 1) is one of the oldest surviving Qurʾānic manuscripts. It is a palimpsest — parchment that has been written, erased, and rewritten — containing two layers of Qurʾānic text:

- **Lower Text (scriptio inferior):** The original, erased text, dated palaeographically to the 7th century CE
- **Upper Text (scriptio superior):** The later, visible text, dated to the 8th century CE

### Discovery

The manuscript was discovered in 1972 during renovation of the Great Mosque of Ṣanʿāʾ, Yemen, in a sealed attic space containing approximately 15,000 manuscript fragments. The cache was transferred to Dār al-Makhṭūṭāt (House of Manuscripts) in Ṣanʿāʾ, where it was catalogued, conserved, and studied by an international team (1981–1989) under the direction of Gerd-R. Puin and Ursula Dreibholz.

### Catalogue Identity

- **Catalogue Number:** DAM 01-27.1
- **Repository:** Dār al-Makhṭūṭāt, Ṣanʿāʾ, Yemen
- **Format:** 38 folios (leaves) of parchment
- **Script:** Hijazi (both upper and lower texts)
- **Lines per page:** Variable (01), line length approximately 27 cm

### Radiocarbon Dating

- **Sample:** Parchment from detached leaf sold at auction
- **Calibrated Range:** 578–669 CE (95% confidence)
- **Laboratory:** ETH Zürich
- **Critical:** Dates the death of the animal, not the writing event. The parchment existed by 578–669 CE, but the lower text was written at some point after the parchment was prepared.

### Palimpsest Structure

The manuscript has been written, erased, and rewritten:

1. **Original Writing (Lower Text):** 7th century CE, Hijazi script, non-standard surah order, significant textual variants
2. **Erasure:** Parchment scraped/washed to remove lower text
3. **Second Writing (Upper Text):** 8th century CE, Hijazi script, largely conforms to standard Uthmanic Quran

### Key Findings

**Upper Text:**
- Largely conforms to standard Uthmanic Quran in text and surah order
- Minor orthographic variants present
- Dated palaeographically to 8th century CE

**Lower Text:**
- Does NOT conform to standard Uthmanic Quran
- Surah order corresponds to NO known Quranic sequence
- Contains significant textual variants: word substitutions, omissions, additions, syntactic rearrangements
- Represents a non-Uthmanic textual tradition
- Estimated 25× more variants than Ibn Masʿūd codex (Sadeghi & Goudarzi 2012)
- 61 variants identified from Cairo 1924 edition in 35 folios (Hilali 2017)

### Scholarly Interpretations

**Sadeghi & Goudarzi (2012):**
- Lower text represents pre-Uthmanic companion codex tradition
- Variants suggest dictation errors and independent transmission
- Historical model: multiple competing codices before Uthmanic standardization

**Hilali (2017):**
- Lower text may be product of "reading circle" or pedagogical exercise
- Both layers show "work in progress" characteristics
- Institutional/pedagogical context rather than canonical codex

**Elizabeth Puin:**
- Lower text is pre-Quranic text not corresponding to 8th–10th century narratives
- All lower-text variants change meaning of text
- Evidence against verbatim preservation

### Critical Conclusions

1. **Physical Independence:** YES — DAM 01-27.1 is a unique physical object
2. **Textual Independence:** YES — Lower text represents independent non-Uthmanic tradition
3. **Genealogical Relationship:** DISPUTED — May descend from companion codex tradition, but NOT ESTABLISHED
4. **Autograph Recovery:** NOT ESTABLISHED — Cannot demonstrate relationship to original revelation
5. **Two Qurʾāns:** DISPUTED — Lower and upper texts are both Quranic but differ significantly; "two Qurʾāns" is coarse formulation
6. **Corruption:** NOT ESTABLISHED — Variants exist, but do not prove deliberate corruption
7. **Perfect Preservation:** NOT ESTABLISHED — Variants demonstrate textual plurality, not perfect uniformity

## E. PALIMPSEST TECHNOLOGY

### Definition

A **palimpsest** is a manuscript page that has been written on, scraped or washed clean, and then used again for a second (or subsequent) writing.

### Process

1. **Original Writing:** Text written on parchment
2. **Erasure:** Parchment scraped or washed to remove ink
3. **Reuse:** Parchment written on again with new text
4. **Potential Repetition:** Process may be repeated multiple times

### Terminology

- **Scriptio Inferior (Lower Text):** The original, erased writing
- **Scriptio Superior (Upper Text):** The later, visible writing
- **Palimpsest:** The physical object containing multiple layers

### Critical Principles

**DO NOT ASSUME:**
- Lower text = earliest Quranic text ever written
- Lower text = original revelation
- Upper text = Uthmanic standard text
- Erasure = corruption
- Erasure = error correction
- Erasure = obsolescence

**ALL REQUIRE EVIDENCE**

### Ṣanʿāʾ Palimpsest Structure

**Lower Text (Scriptio Inferior):**
- Original writing on parchment
- 7th century CE (palaeographic)
- Hijazi script
- Non-standard surah order
- Significant textual variants
- Erased by scraping/washing

**Erasure:**
- Parchment scraped or washed
- Lower text partially visible under UV light
- Traces remain for digital reconstruction

**Upper Text (Scriptio Superior):**
- Second writing on same parchment
- 8th century CE (palaeographic)
- Hijazi script
- Largely conforms to standard Uthmanic Quran
- Visible in natural light

### Why Was Lower Text Erased?

**Possible Reasons (NOT ESTABLISHED):**

1. **Text Faded:** Lower text had become illegible
2. **Text Inaccurate:** Lower text contained errors requiring correction
3. **Text Obsolete:** Lower text was superseded by standard version
4. **Text Nascent:** Lower text was draft or exercise, not canonical
5. **Parchment Reuse:** Economic reasons (parchment expensive)
6. **Institutional Change:** New teaching context required new text

**Critical:** The reason for erasure is NOT ESTABLISHED. Multiple hypotheses exist.

### Palimpsest ≠ Proof of Two Qurʾāns

**Logical Error:**
- Palimpsest shows two layers of text
- Therefore, two different Qurʾāns existed
- Therefore, corruption occurred

**Why This Is Invalid:**
1. Two layers do not necessarily represent two complete Qurʾāns
2. Lower text is fragmentary (not complete Quran)
3. Upper text is also fragmentary
4. Both layers are Quranic (same religious corpus)
5. Differences may represent variants, not different scriptures
6. "Two Qurʾāns" requires definition and evidence

**Correct Interpretation:**
- Palimpsest shows two textual layers
- Lower text represents one textual tradition
- Upper text represents another textual tradition
- Both are Quranic but differ in detail
- Relationship to "original" is NOT ESTABLISHED

## T. PRESERVATION CLAIM AUDIT

### The Claim

**Claim:** "Ṣanʿāʾ proves perfect Qurʾānic preservation."

**Source:** Apologetic literature

### Distinguishing Levels

**1. Substantial Continuity:**
- Core text preserved

**2. Consonantal Continuity:**

- **Status:** SUPPORTED (upper text conforms to Uthmanic rasm)

- Spelling conventions preserved
- **Status:** PARTIALLY SUPPORTED (minor orthographic variants)

- Qirāʾāt (reading traditions) preserved
- **Status:** NOT ESTABLISHED (lower text may represent different reading)

**5. Textual Uniformity:**
- All manuscripts identical
- **Status:** NOT SUPPORTED (lower text differs significantly)

**6. Complete Historical Identity:**
- Text identical throughout history
- **Status:** NOT SUPPORTED (lower text differs from upper text)

**7. Exact Autograph Recovery:**
- Original text perfectly recovered
- **Status:** NOT ESTABLISHED (relationship to autograph unknown)

**8. Theological Preservation:**
- Theological meaning preserved
- **Status:** PARTIALLY SUPPORTED (core theology preserved, some variants change meaning)

### Critical Assessment

**Ṣanʿāʾ Evidence:**

**Upper Text:**
- Conforms to standard Uthmanic Quran
- Supports substantial continuity
- **Status:** SUPPORTS PRESERVATION (upper text)

**Lower Text:**
- Does NOT conform to standard
- Demonstrates textual plurality
- **Status:** CHALLENGES PERFECT PRESERVATION (lower text)

**Palimpsest Structure:**
- Shows textual change over time
- Lower text erased, upper text written
- **Status:** DEMONSTRATES TEXTUAL DEVELOPMENT

### Critical Conclusion

**DO NOT CLAIM:**
- "Ṣanʿāʾ proves perfect preservation"
- "Ṣanʿāʾ disproves preservation"
- "Textual plurality disproves perfect preservation" (this conflates manuscript variation with theological disproof)

**CORRECT INTERPRETATION:**
- Ṣanʿāʾ demonstrates evidence of early textual variation, but the manuscript alone does not settle theological claims about "perfect preservation"
- Such claims require an explicit definition of preservation and a specified textual/transmission model
- Upper text supports substantial continuity with later standardized tradition
- Lower text demonstrates textual plurality in early transmission
- Perfect preservation (as complete manuscript uniformity) NOT ESTABLISHED
- Substantial continuity SUPPORTED

**Distinguish:**
- (A) Textual preservation claim (text remained stable over time)
- (B) Manuscript uniformity claim (all manuscripts identical)
- (C) Rasm preservation claim (consonantal skeleton preserved)
- (D) Oral transmission claim (oral tradition preserved text)

**Evidence Status:**
- (A) Textual preservation: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED (upper text conforms, lower text differs)
- (B) Manuscript uniformity: NOT SUPPORTED (lower text differs)
- (C) Rasm preservation: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED (upper text conforms to Uthmanic rasm type)
- (D) Oral transmission: NOT ESTABLISHED by manuscript evidence alone
- (E) Theological preservation: OUTSIDE SCOPE of manuscript analysis

**Status:** MIXED EVIDENCE — substantial continuity demonstrated, perfect preservation (as uniformity) not demonstrated, theological claims outside manuscript scope
