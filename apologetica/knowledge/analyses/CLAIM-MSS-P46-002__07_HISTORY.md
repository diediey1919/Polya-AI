# Analysis 07_HISTORY: P46 Historical Pauline Transmission
P46 provides essential historical evidence that the Pauline corpus was being copied, preserved, and transmitted as a substantial collection by c. 175–225 CE, predating the major 4th-century uncial codices (Sinaiticus, Vaticanus) by over a century. This demonstrates early Christian textual culture and the importance of Pauline writings within early Christian communities.
