## 0. SCOPE, METHOD, AND EPISTEMIC DISCIPLINE

This audit treats kalam simultaneously as (a) a historical intellectual tradition, (b) a set of competing theological methodologies, and (c) a collection of arguments whose premises require independent logical evaluation. Five questions are kept formally separate for every proposition P:

1. **HISTORICAL:** Did school S hold P at layer L? (evidence: texts, transmission, dating)
2. **TEXTUAL:** What Qur'anic/hadith wording grounds P, and what linguistic work connects wording to P?
3. **PHILOSOPHICAL:** What metaphysical assumptions does P presuppose?
4. **LOGICAL:** Is the argument for P valid, and are its premises true?
5. **SUNNI THEOLOGICAL:** How does established Sunni aqidah assess P?

Conflation of any two is classified as a methodological error (see Error Register E1–E20).

**Source control:** No new SRC-* records are created. All novel claims below are second-order syntheses over (i) the existing store (Qur'anic manuscript/transmission base, STEP 101–112; early-Islam historiography, STEP 113–121), and (ii) the publicly established doxographical record (al-Ash'ari, *Maqalat al-Islamiyyin*; al-Baghdadi, *al-Farq bayn al-Firaq*; al-Shahrastani, *al-Milal wa-l-Nihal*; Ibn al-Nadim, *al-Fihrist*; Qadi 'Abd al-Jabbar, *al-Mughni*; al-Juwayni, *al-Irshad*; al-Ghazali, *Tahafut*; Ibn Taymiyyah, *Dar' Ta'arud*, *Minhaj al-Sunnah*; modern scholarship: Wolfson, Frank, van Ess, Gimaret, McDermott, Madelung, Wisnovsky, Adamson). Classical-work citations here function as scholarly references for a synthesis audit, NOT as newly ingested primary witnesses; where a classical report's transmission is itself disputed, confidence is capped at PROBABLE and the limitation is stated. Nothing is quoted as verbatim primary text unless the wording is independently established; paraphrase is labelled as paraphrase.

## E. WHAT IS KALAM? (DEFINITION AUDIT)

Three definition-strata are separated; no modern definition is retrojected.

**E1. Early usage (L6–L9):** *kalam* ≈ disciplined disputation (*jadal*, *munazarah*) about God, His speech, decree, and the sinner — often pejorative among traditionists ("*ahl al-kalam*"). The term marks a METHOD (dialectical reasoning on doctrinal questions), not yet a guild. [L6–L9, PROBABLE]
**E2. Later self-definition (L11–L18):** al-Ghazali (*Mustasfa* logic prolegomenon), al-Iji (*Mawaqif*): *'ilm al-kalam* = the science establishing religious beliefs (*'aqa'id diniyyah*) by certain proofs (*adillah yaqiniyyah*) and answering doubts — i.e., systematic/defensive theology. al-Taftazani adds explicit defense-against-opponents framing. [L17–L19, ESTABLISHED as later formulation; NOT retrojected to L6]
**E3. Modern academic (L20):** Wolfson ("philosophy of the Kalam" — atomist occasionalism), Frank (kalam as autonomous tradition, not failed philosophy), van Ess (theology + social history), Gimaret (doctrine-by-doctrine). Modern category "rational theology" is an etic convenience. [L20]

**Finding:** the four candidate glosses — dialectical theology (E1), rational theology (E1–E2), defense of revelation (E2), systematic theology (E2–E3) — are all legitimate AT THEIR OWN LAYER and contradictory only when one layer's definition is imposed on another. Claim B ("no connection whatsoever to pre-Islamic traditions") and counter-reduction "kalam = Greek philosophy" both fail (see AL-B, AL-Z, AN-E2).

## F. ORIGINS OF KALAM (INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL)

**Internal drivers (necessary; jointly sufficient for emergence):**

1. **Qur'anic reasoning itself (L1):** the Qur'an argues — *istidlal* (signs/*ayat*), challenge (*tahaddi*), reductio against association (*law kana fihima alihatun illa Allah la-fasadata*, Q 21:22), rhetorical disputation with opponents. Qur'anic argument-forms predate any translation. [L1, ESTABLISHED]
2. **Prophetic disputation (L1–L2):** reported debates (e.g., Najran delegation, Q 3:61 *mubahalah*; cf. STEP 113/114 controls on report-vs-event). [L1–L2, PROBABLE at report level]
3. **Political controversies (L2/L4):** Siffin arbitration → Khawarij ("no judgment but God's") → status-of-sinner → faith-vs-works. The FIRST systematic theological propositions in Islam concern the sinner and the ruler, not metaphysics. [L2–L4, ESTABLISHED]
4. **Qadar controversy (L3):** Ma'bad/Ghaylan affirm human *qudrah* against Umayyad quietism; counter-reports affirm decree. Both poles are intra-Islamic before any documented Greek mediation. [L3, PROBABLE]
5. **Divine-attributes pressure (L5–L6):** Jahm b. Safwan (d. 128/745; radical *ta'til*), Muqatil b. Sulayman (d. 150/767; anthropomorphic *tashbih* reports) — the extremes that forced a middle vocabulary. [L5–L6, PROBABLE]
6. **Sectarian debate literature (L5–L6):** *firaq* polemics generate the question-list kalam systematizes. [L5–L6]

**External interactions (modulating; NOT originating):**

7. **Christian/Jewish disputation (L5–L7):** John of Damascus (*De Haeresibus* ch. 100 on "Ishmaelites"), Theodore Abu Qurrah, Hunayn-era apologetics; Trinity/incarnation polemics sharpen Islamic divine-unity argument. Documented contact, but direction-of-influence per thesis requires independent evidence. [L5–L7, PROBABLE contact; specific borrowings mostly DISPUTED]
8. **Greek/Syriac material (L7):** logic (*mantiq*), categories, *jawhar/'arad* (substance/accident) vocabulary, atomist physics parallels (Democritean/Indian parallels also proposed — origin of kalam atomism is DISPUTED, not settled Greek borrowing). Enters AFTER kalam's core questions exist; reshapes method more than agenda. [L7, ESTABLISHED presence; borrowing-theses mostly PROBABLE-to-DISPUTED per case]

**Synthesis:** internal development is the engine; external traditions are fuel and tooling. Single-origin theories (purely internal OR purely derivative) are UNSAFE (AL-A vs AL-B; AN-E2).

## G. REASON AND REVELATION (EPISTEMIC HIERARCHY PER SCHOOL)

Seven diagnostic questions (Q1–Q7) put to each school. Abbreviations: R = reason ('aql), W = revelation (wahy/naql), S = sense (hiss), K = report (khabar/tawatur).

**G1. Mu'tazila (L6–L8; systematized by Qadi 'Abd al-Jabbar, d. 415/1025, *al-Mughni*).** Q1: R knows God's existence, unity, justice, obligation of gratitude (*shukr al-mun'im*), good/evil of acts — independently (doctrine of *al-'aql yahkum*). Q2: W required for ritual detail (*shar'iyyat*: prayer forms, measures), eschatological particulars, confirmation. Q3: Yes — *dalil al-huduth*. Q4: Yes — negative attributes (no likeness) and justice-content. Q5: Yes — R can establish the need for and truthfulness conditions of prophecy (veracity + miracle), though the FACT of a given prophet comes by K. Q6: Yes — moral rationalism (*al-tahsin wa-l-taqbih al-'aqliyyan*). Q7: R never "overrides" W properly understood; but where literal sense entails impossibility (*muhal*: likeness, injustice), R compels *ta'wil*. Hierarchy: R foundational (*asl*), W superstructure (*far'*); W's authority is itself R-certified. [L6–L8]
**G2. Athari/traditionalist (L9; Ahmad b. Hanbal; later Ibn Taymiyyah L-post-19, Wahhabi/Salafi L20).** Q1: R knows basic truths (existence, lordship) via *fitrah* + signs, but obligation and detail come by W. Q2: nearly everything doctrinally decisive requires W. Q3: Yes, but Qur'anic-sign reasoning, not autonomous metaphysics. Q4: Only affirmatively as W affirms (*ithbat ma athbatahu Allah li-nafsihi*); modality unknown (*bi-la kayf*). Q5: Prophecy known by W-internal evidence (character, miracle, content) — R assists, never judges W. Q6: No independent moral knowledge binding God; good/evil known via command (divine-command orientation; cf. section P). Q7: Never — *tafwid* (consign modality) or affirmation-without-likeness replaces *ta'wil*. Hierarchy: W supreme; R is servant (*khadem*), witness, and refuter of sophistry. [L9]
**G3. Ash'ari (L10–L11; mature Juwayni/Ghazali L17; Razi L18).** Q1: R establishes existence (*huduth/imkan*), unity in the sense excluding rivals, and the POSSIBILITY-framework; obligation to believe arises only after W arrives (*shukr* not R-obligatory pre-revelation — anti-Mu'tazili point). Q2: attributes beyond existence/power/knowledge/will/life/speech/hearing/seeing, all eschatology, ritual law. Q3: Yes. Q4: Partially — the seven/eight *sifat ma'nawiyyah* rationally; *khabariyyah* (face/hand/istiwa') by W with *tafwid* (early) or *ta'wil* (later). Q5: Yes — miracle-based proof (*dalil al-mu'jizah*). Q6: No — moral properties fixed by command; R discerns benefit (*maslahah*) but not binding good/evil (divine-command; cf. P). Q7: Yes, constrained — apparent anthropomorphism is reinterpreted or consigned, because impossibility cannot be W's intent. Hierarchy: R proves W's credentials; W then outranks R in content. [L10–L18]
**G4. Maturidi (L12–L13; *Kitab al-Tawhid*; Nasafi *'Aqa'id*).** Q1: R knows God, unity, and BASIC moral truths (injustice ugly, truthfulness good) — closer to Mu'tazila than Ash'ari on Q6-partial: R grasps good/evil (*husn/qubh*) in essence though obligation (*wujub*) to act is completed by W. Q2: ritual/eschatological detail, exact recompense. Q3–Q5: Yes (huduth/imkan; attributes; miracle-prophecy). Q7: Yes, constrained like Ash'ari but with stronger confidence in R's moral perception. Hierarchy: R and W harmonious; R independent in foundations, W decisive in details; unlike Mu'tazila, W is not R's "product." [L12–L13]
**G5. Imami (L14; Mufid/Murtada/Tusi).** Close to Mu'tazili epistemology (Baghdad school cross-fertilization, L14): R knows existence, unity, justice, *lutf* (gracious necessity incl. Imamate); W (Qur'an + Imam-transmitted Sunnah) gives detail. Distinctive: the Imam as living epistemic guarantor extends W's function post-Prophet. [L14]
**G6. Zaydi (L15).** Mu'tazili-adjacent rationalism (Basran influence via Yemeni/Caspian lines): R knows unity, justice, obligation; W details; reason judges anthropomorphic literalism out. Imamate rationally indicated but Imam is present/fallible-scholarly, not hidden-infallible. [L15]
**G7. Ismaili (L16; Sijistani, Kirmani).** Two-tier epistemology: *zahir* (literal, W-level, for the many) vs *batin* (intellective reality via *ta'wil*, graded by hierarchy: *natiq–asas–imam–hujjah–da'i*). R-as-intellect (*'aql*) ascends emanative hierarchy; W's letter is pedagogical. Outlier: reason does not merely certify W, it TRANSCENDS the letter. [L16]

Summary matrix (role of reason): Mu'tazili = judge; Athari = servant-witness; Ash'ari = certifier-then-subordinate; Maturidi = partner; Imami = judge-with-Imam; Zaydi = judge-with-present-Imam; Ismaili = transcender. (Full matrix: section AO.)

## H. MU'TAZILA (L6–L8)

**H1. Formation.** Basra circle: Wasil b. 'Ata' (d. 131/748; *manzilah* doctrine; withdrew — *i'tazala* — from Hasan al-Basri's circle over the grave sinner [L6]); 'Amr b. 'Ubayd (d. 144/761; ascetic-traditionist bridge). Systematizers: Abu al-Hudhayl al-'Allaf (d. 235/849; atomist-attribute theory — God's attributes = His essence; *'ilm = qudrah = dhat*), al-Nazzam (d. 231/845; radical motion-theory, denial of atom, *tawarrud*/leap; most speculative), Bishr b. al-Mu'tamir (d. 210/825; Baghdad school founder; *tawallud* — generated acts), al-Jahiz (d. 255/868; literary theologian; *Kitab al-Hayawan*; divine justice via natural order), Qadi 'Abd al-Jabbar (d. 415/1025; *al-Mughni* encyclopedia; mature Basran synthesis). Two branches: Basra (stricter unity/justice) vs Baghdad (concessions: e.g., BGB Baghdadis allow God's will as distinct, allow intercession-scope differences). [L6–L8]

**H2. Core doctrines (non-uniform; variation noted per thesis).**

1. **Tawhid:** absolute unity excludes distinct eternal attributes; attributes are identical with essence (*sifat = dhat*); denial of anthropomorphic literalism; denial of eternal Qur'an (see J). Basra stricter than Baghdad. [L6–L8]
2. **Divine justice ('adl):** God cannot do injustice (*zulm*) or will evil; He MUST do the optimum (*al-aslah*) for creatures (Basran *aslah*-obligation; Baghdad weaker); suffering requires compensation (*'iwad*). [L6–L8]
3. **Created Qur'an:** God's speech is an act (*fi'l*), hence originated (*muhdath*), not an eternal attribute (see J). [L7–L8]
4. **Human freedom:** humans create (*khalq*) their own acts; divine foreknowledge is of what the agent WILL freely do (no causal pre-determination); *tawallud* debate on indirect effects. [L6–L8]
5. **Moral rationalism:** acts bear intrinsic good/evil knowable by R prior to command (see P). [L6–L8]
6. **Promise and threat (*wa'd/wa'id*):** God MUST reward obedience and punish unrepentant grave sin (denial of intercession-absolution for unrepentant *kabira*; threat is binding). [L6–L8]
7. **Intermediate position (*manzilah bayn al-manzilatayn*):** grave sinner is neither believer (*mu'min*) nor unbeliever (*kafir*) but *fasiq*, eternally in Fire unrepented — the founding thesis (Wasil). [L6]
8. **Rational obligation:** gratitude to the Benefactor, reflection (*nazar*) leading to knowledge of God are R-obligatory even pre-revelation. [L6–L8]

**H3. Non-uniformity control:** Nazzam vs Abu al-Hudhayl on atomism; Basra vs Baghdad on *aslah*/will/intercession; Jahiz's naturalism vs Qadi's systematics. Any "the Mu'tazila held X" without branch-tag is capped at PROBABLE (AL-D; AN-E4).

## I. FIVE PRINCIPLES (*AL-USUL AL-KHAMSAH*)

Traditional enumeration: (1) *al-Tawhid*, (2) *al-'Adl*, (3) *al-Wa'd wa-l-Wa'id*, (4) *al-Manzilah bayn al-Manzilatayn*, (5) *al-Amr bi-l-Ma'ruf wa-l-Nahy 'an al-Munkar* (commanding right/forbidding wrong — incl. the duty, under conditions, to oppose unjust rule; Kharijite-adjacent political edge, systematized away from rebellion-anarchism).

- **Early formulation (L6):** Wasil's circle centers (4) + (1) + (2); (5) functions as communal ethic; the five as a CANONICAL LIST is not demonstrably Wasil's own — earliest secure fivefold enumerations postdate the founders. [L6, PROBABLE for content; NOT ESTABLISHED as original pentad]
- **Later systematization (L8–L10):** Baghdad/Basra creeds (e.g., Abu 'Ali al-Jubba'i d. 303/915; Qadi 'Abd al-Jabbar's *Sharh al-Usul al-Khamsah*) fix the five as identity-markers, each with sub-theses and proof-chains. [L8–L10, ESTABLISHED]
- **Internal variation:** *aslah* scope (Basra strong/Baghdad weak); *wa'id* exceptions (repentance, *ihbat*/cancellation mechanics differ); *manzilah* eschatology unanimous but its *iman*-definition contested; (5)'s political entailment ranges from quietist-preaching to legitimized revolt (Zaydi-adjacent edge). [L6–L15]

## J. QUR'AN CREATEDNESS (DEDICATED FORENSIC AUDIT)

Six referents separated before any verdict (equivocation here caused the historical shouting):

- (a) Divine speech as attribute/act; (b) the revealed Qur'an as meaningful speech (*kalam lafzi ma'nawi*); (c) the written mushaf (ink, paper, binding); (d) recitation-act (*qira'ah* as human act vs recited content); (e) eternal divine attribute (*sifah qadimah*); (f) originated expression (*lafz muhdath*).

**J1. Mu'tazili (L7–L8):** God's speech = act of creating meaningful sounds/letters; hence originated; an eternal Qur'an would be a second eternal (*qidam* plurality violates tawhid). The mushaf/ink AND the recited wording are created. Driving logic: unity + divine-freedom (God SPEAKS when He wills). [L7–L8]
**J2. Athari/traditionalist (L8–L9):** "The Qur'an is God's speech, uncreated (*ghayr makhluq*), wherever recited/written/memorized." Ahmad b. Hanbal (Mihnah): refusal to say "created" is confessional boundary; later Athari formula distinguishes the RECITATION-ACT (human, created) from the RECITED (divine, uncreated) — *al-qira'ah makhluqah wa-l-maqru' ghayr makhluq* (al-Bukhari's *Khalq Af'al al-'Ibad* nuance). (c)/(d-act) created; (b)/(e) uncreated. [L8–L9]
**J3. Ash'ari (L10–L11):** Two-level: *kalam nafsi* (internal, eternal, undivided meaning subsisting in essence) vs *kalam lafzi* (originated expression: letters, sounds, mushaf). The Qur'an-as-eternal = the *nafsi* meaning; the Arabic wording/mushaf = created indicator (*dalalah*). Solves tawhid (no plural eternals — only one eternal meaning) + revelation (a real originated guide). Critics (Mu'tazila: invented entity; Athari: splits what Salaf affirmed simply). [L10–L11]
**J4. Maturidi (L12–L13):** Near-Ash'ari: eternal speech subsisting in essence; expression originated. Maturidi stress: speech as genuine attribute of perfection (against Mu'tazili reduction to act), with Hanafi-*fiqh* caution about pronouncing *takfir* over wording disputes. [L12–L13]
**J5. Imami (L14):** Majority: Qur'an created/originated (*muhdath*) — Mu'tazili-adjacent (Murtada/Tusi); minority traditionist reports affirm uncreatedness ( Patrimonio hadith "*al-Qur'an kalam Allah ghayr makhluq*" exists in Imami collections but rationalist usulis marginalize it). Branch-tag mandatory. [L14]
**J6. Zaydi (L15):** Created-Qur'an position standard (Mu'tazili method inheritance); same attribute/act logic as J1. [L15]
**J7. Ismaili (L16):** The letter (*lafz*) belongs to the *zahir* level, instituted through the *natiq* (speaking prophet); ultimate reality (*haqa'iq*) transcends letter/attribute binaries. Createdness affirmed at letter-level; the question is DISSOLVED upward rather than answered at the same level. [L16]

**Boundary verdict:** the slogan "created vs uncreated" is equivocal until referents (a)–(f) are fixed; with referents fixed, positions J1–J7 are mutually intelligible and the dispute is substantive (metaphysics of speech + tawhid + freedom), not merely verbal (AL-F; case study 5).

## K. DIVINE ATTRIBUTES (DEDICATED AUDIT)

Thirteen loci: knowledge (*'ilm*), power (*qudrah*), will (*iradah/mashi'ah*), life (*hayah*), hearing (*sam'*), seeing (*basar*), speech (*kalam*), mercy (*rahmah*), anger (*ghadab*), face (*wajh*), hand (*yad*), *istiwa'* (Q 20:5), descent (*nuzul*; hadith "yanzilu rabbuna").

Five method-labels: **Ithbat** (affirm wording), **Tafwid** (affirm wording, consign modality/meaning-specification to God), **Ta'wil** (redirect wording to a licensed sense: power, dominion, mercy-act), **Tashbih** (likening to creatures — condemned by all schools as doctrine; accusation-label in polemics), **Tanzih** (incomparability: Q 42:11 *laysa ka-mithlihi shay'*).

**K1. Mu'tazili:** essence-identity (*sifat = dhat*); knowledge/power = essence modalized by objects; will = act (commands originate); hearing/seeing = knowledge of audibles/visibles (no distinct faculties); speech = created act (J1); mercy/anger = acts of benefit/punishment (no psychic states); face = essence (*wajh* = *dhat*, Q 55:27); hand = favor/power (*ni'mah/qudrah*); *istiwa'* = dominion (*istila'* — the famous Bishr/poetic reading); descent = act of mercy/command. TEXTUAL basis: *muhkamat* of tanzih rule the *mutashabihat*; LINGUISTIC method: established Arabic metaphor-licenses; ASSUMPTION: distinct eternals violate tawhid; LOGICAL concern: composition/multiplicity in the Eternal. [L6–L8]
**K2. Athari:** affirm ALL *bila kayf wa-la tashbih wa-la ta'til* (without modality, likeness, or nullification); *istiwa'* affirmed as revealed, modality unknown, asking "how" is innovation (*al-kayf majhul* — Malik report, transmission DISPUTED in wording but early in sense); descent affirmed as reported; mercy/anger affirmed as befitting Him. TEXTUAL: literal wording is itself the datum; LINGUISTIC: ordinary sense retained, creaturely *mode* denied; ASSUMPTION: Salaf consensus + W's self-description suffices; LOGICAL concern: *ta'wil* licenses unbelief-by-degrees. [L9]
**K3. Ash'ari:** seven/eight *ma'nawiyyah* (knowledge, power, will, life, hearing, seeing, speech + *idrak*/perception disputed) = real distinct attributes subsisting in essence, neither identical to essence (vs Mu'tazila) nor "other than" Him in a separable sense (*la huwa wa-la ghayruhu*); *khabariyyah* (face/hand/istiwa'/descent/eye/fingers): EARLY Ash'ari (*Ibanah* layer) affirms with *tafwid*; LATER Ash'ari (Juwayni→Razi) prefers *ta'wil* (hand=power, *istiwa'*=dominion, descent=mercy-act). Layer-tag mandatory (AL-J; AN-E8). TEXTUAL: *muhkam* reason-rules + *khabar* wording; LINGUISTIC: attribute-terms are real (*haqiqah*) for *ma'nawiyyah*, licensed-metaphor for *khabariyyah* (later layer); ASSUMPTION: perfection requires real attributes; negation empties lordship; LOGICAL concern: block composition by *la huwa wa-la ghayruhu*. [L10–L18]
**K4. Maturidi:** near-Ash'ari on *ma'nawiyyah* plus *takwin* (originating/creating) as a DISTINCT eternal attribute (Ash'ari reduces *takwin* to power's effect — the famous *sifat al-takwin* dispute); *khabariyyah* handled by *tafwid*-leaning restraint with *ta'wil* permitted. Also affirms eight: adds *takwin*. [L12–L13]
**K5. Imami:** Mu'tazili-adjacent essence-identity for *ma'nawiyyah*; *khabariyyah* by *ta'wil*; plus *'isma*-adjacent perfection logic (God's acts always wise/just). [L14]
**K6. Zaydi:** as K5 minus Imam-'isma extension. [L15]
**K7. Ismaili:** radical *tanzih*: even "God is knowing/powerful" is pedagogical; ultimate Source beyond affirmation AND negation (*la ... wa-la ...* double-negation ladders, Sijistani); *ta'wil* applies to the entire *zahir* including attribute verses. [L16]

## L. TAWHID (COMPETING FORMULATIONS — NOT IDENTICAL)

- **Mu'tazili tawhid (L6–L8):** numerical + qualitative simplicity: no distinct eternal attributes, no eternal speech, no anthropomorphic remainder; unity = essence-identity + justice-unity (God's acts one in wisdom). Strongest metaphysical simplicity claim in Islamic theology.
- **Athari tawhid (L9):** unity of lordship/worship/names-attributes (*tawhid al-rububiyyah/uluhiyyah/asma' wa-sifat* in later systematization, L-post-19 Ibn Taymiyyah layer; early layer: *tawhid* as Qur'anic confession + Salafi *ithbat*). Unity COMPATIBLE with real distinct attributes; multiplicity of attributes ≠ multiplicity of gods because attributes subsist, not rival.
- **Ash'ari tawhid (L10–L18):** unity = no partner in essence, attributes, or acts (*la sharika lahu fi dhatihi wa-la fi sifatihi wa-la fi af'alihi*) — compatible with *la huwa wa-la ghayruhu* attributes and with divine monopoly on creating (*khalq* of all acts; cf. N/O).
- **Maturidi tawhid (L12–L13):** as Ash'ari + *takwin* unity (one origination-attribute); stronger emphasis that unity is R-known.
- **Imami tawhid (L14):** Mu'tazili simplicity + *tawhid al-af'al* stress (no independent agent) + Imamate coherence (divine wisdom requires *lutf*/guide).
- **Zaydi tawhid (L15):** as Imami minus hidden-Imam entailments.
- **Ismaili transcendence (L16):** unity beyond unity: the Source is beyond "one" as number/attribute; *tawhid* = the intellect's ascent-stripping (*tanzih murakkab*).

"Same word, different theories" — equivocation across these is a category error (AL; AN-E4/E7–E13).

## M. DIVINE JUSTICE (DOES GOD HAVE OBLIGATIONS?)

Three questions: (i) Can R know good/evil? (ii) Can God do *zulm*? (iii) Does God OWE creatures anything?

- **Mu'tazili (L6–L8):** (i) Yes. (ii) No — impossible (*mustahil*), not merely unactual; injustice contradicts wisdom. (iii) Yes-analogue: *aslah* (optimum, Basra), compensation (*'iwad*) for unmerited suffering, binding promise/threat. Obligation-language is analogical (*wajib 'ala Allah* = wisdom necessitates), not creature-over-Creator command.
- **Ash'ari (L10–L18):** (i) No independent binding moral knowledge (P). (ii) God CANNOT be unjust by definition: injustice = violating another's right/property; all is His property, so no act of His is *zulm*; "God punishing the obedient" is POSSIBLE but He informed He won't (W-certainty replaces R-necessity). (iii) No obligations; *lutf* is grace, not debt. Logic: preserve omnipotence + transcendence; cost: Mu'tazili charge of moral voluntarism.
- **Maturidi (L12–L13):** middle: (i) R knows basic good/evil (vs Ash'ari); (ii) God does not act unjustly — wisdom (*hikmah*) characterizes Him though not as Basran *aslah*-necessity; (iii) no strict obligation, but divine wisdom makes injustice inconceivable-in-character (*laysa min sha'nihi*).
- **Athari (L9):** God is just because He SAYS so and DOES so; R does not legislate for Him; Q 21:23 (*la yus'alu 'amma yaf'alu*) is the stop-sign; *qadar*-content accepted as wise even where R cannot see wisdom.
- **Imami/Zaydi (L14–L15):** Mu'tazili-adjacent (i)–(iii) + *lutf*-necessity (Imamate/prophecy as gracious requirements of justice).
- **Ismaili (L16):** justice reframed cosmologically: hierarchy/retribution are intellective order, not juridical obligation.

## N. FREE WILL AND QADAR (COMPETING MODELS, NO CARICATURE)

Six positions on one spectrum (divine causation ↔ human agency):

1. **Jabri/Jahmite pole (L5):** Jahm b. Safwan — human acts are compelled (*majbur*); power (*qudrah*) is epiphenomenal; "human acts like tree movements." Rejected by ALL later schools; retained as foil. [L5]
2. **Mu'tazili libertarianism (L6–L8):** humans CREATE their acts (*khaliq li-af'alihi* in the originated sense); divine will does not causally determine obedience/disobedience; foreknowledge is non-causal. *Tawallud* (indirect effects, Bishr) extends responsibility. [L6–L8]
3. **Ash'ari acquisition (L10–L18):** God creates ALL originated things incl. human acts; the human "acquires" (*iktisab*) the act via originated power/will concomitant with it. See O. [L10–L18]
4. **Maturidi agency (L12–L13):** human acts occur by human originated power (*qudrah hadithah*) with real choice (*ikhtiyar*); God creates the ACT's existence while the human's "doing-ascription" (*fi'liyyah*) is genuinely theirs — stronger agency-language than Ash'ari *kasb*, weaker than Mu'tazili creation. Two-aspect model (*jihatan*). [L12–L13]
5. **Athari (L9):** affirm BOTH decree-texts and agency-texts (*tawfiq* + *khidhlan*); refuse systematizing metaphysics; agency is real because W says "you do," decree real because W says "God decreed"; the HOW is consigned. Not a "model" in kalam's sense — a boundary discipline. [L9]
6. **Imami/Zaydi (L14–L15):** *al-amr bayn al-amrayn* ("the matter between the two matters" — neither compulsion nor delegation): Ja'fari formula mediating Jabrite/Qadarite poles; Imami rationalists (Murtada) develop it in Mu'tazili-adjacent libertarian direction while retaining the formula. [L14–L15]

Secondary-causation note: all except the Jahmite pole affirm SOME human efficacy; disagreement is metaphysical (creation vs acquisition vs ascription), not over whether courts may punish (all affirm responsibility).

## O. KASB (DEDICATED AUDIT — NOT "NO FREE WILL")

**O1. What Ash'ari *kasb* means.** *Kasb/iktisab* = the relation between the human agent (endowed with originated power *qudrah hadithah* + will + knowledge, created in him by God) and the act God creates *at* (*'inda*) that power, not *by* (*bi-*) it. The act is God's creation (*khalq*) and the human's acquisition (*kasb*) simultaneously — two descriptions, one event. Responsibility attaches to acquisition. Baqillani/Juwayni refinements: the originated power has a real (if causally dependent) role in SPECIFYING which act occurs; hence praise/blame are non-arbitrary. [L10–L17]
**O2. Does it preserve meaningful agency?** Internal Ash'ari answer: yes — acquisition + intention + commanded-prohibition structure = full moral personality; God commands only the acquirable. Critics' charge (see O3) is EXTERNAL. Neutral audit note: *kasb* preserves agency *within* an occasionalist metaphysics (S); transplanting libertarian criteria into it begs the question, and vice versa. [L10–L18]
**O3. How critics interpret it.** Mu'tazila: disguised compulsion (*jabr muqanna'*); "concomitance without efficacy = compulsion with good manners." Philosophers (Ibn Rushd, *Kashf*): destroys wisdom/purpose in creation. Modern academics (some): occasionalism leaves *kasb* a legal fiction. [L8/L17–L20]
**O4. Ash'ari responses.** (a) Efficacy-objection assumes the Mu'tazili definition of agency (act-creation); Ash'ari defines agency as qualified acquisition — the dispute is definitional, and definitions are not refutations. (b) Qur'anic ascription-texts ("*bi-ma kasabat aydikum*," Q 42:30) license the category textually. (c) Occasionalism is independently argued (S), not assumed to save *kasb*. (d) Maturidi two-aspect model (N4) shows Sunni-kalam-internal resources for strengthening agency without conceding Mu'tazili creation. [L11–L18]

Verdict: "Ash'aris deny free will" is UNSAFE (AL-T; AN-E14). The accurate claim: Ash'aris deny act-CREATION by humans while affirming acquisition-responsibility — a different theory of agency, to be evaluated, not caricatured.

## P. MORAL EPISTEMOLOGY

**P1. Mu'tazili moral rationalism:** acts possess intrinsic *husn/qubh* (goodness/badness) from "faces" (*wujuh*: e.g., lying's corruption of trust-benefit, justice's order-benefit); R perceives these necessarily (*daruri*) in clear cases; hence gratitude, truthfulness-obligation, injustice-prohibition bind pre-revelation; W CONFIRMS and DETAILS. Arguments: (i) pre-revelation accountability intuition; (ii) prophetic-veracity precondition (trusting a prophet's truthfulness presupposes R-known value of truth); (iii) cross-civilizational convergence on basics. [L6–L8]
**P2. Ash'ari divine-command orientation:** no act is good/evil PRIOR to divine address; "good" = commanded/praised, "evil" = prohibited/blamed; R perceives benefit/harm (*masalih*) but not BINDING value; pre-revelation humans are unaccountable (*ahl al-fatrah* excused). Arguments: (i) moral disagreement shows non-necessity; (ii) bindingness needs a Binder; (iii) Q 17:15 (*wa-ma kunna mu'adhdhibina hatta nab'atha rasulan*). [L10–L18]
**P3. Maturidi middle:** R perceives *husn/qubh* in essence (vs Ash'ari) but *wujub* (binding obligation + threat) requires W (vs Mu'tazili) — perception without legislation. [L12–L13]
**P4. Athari:** moral knowledge comes via W + *fitrah*; R's "moral intuitions" are real but fallible and non-legislative; the Prophet's character (*khuluq*) exhibits, not proves, the good. [L9]
**P5. Imami/Zaydi:** P1-adjacent; *lutf*/justice logic presupposes rational moral realism. [L14–L15]
**P6. Sunni critique of P1 (AI/AJ):** (i) faces (*wujuh*) shift with context — same act-type good/evil across cases undermines intrinsicality; (ii) *aslah*-obligation anthropomorphizes wisdom into need; (iii) veracity-precondition argument proves R knows TRUTH-VALUE, not that God is BOUND. Kalam replies (AK): intrinsicality is *ceteris paribus*, not exceptionless; obligation-language is wisdom-necessity, not external command. Verdict on P1 vs P2 is THEOLOGICAL/PHILOSOPHICAL, not historical (AP). [L8–L18]

## Q. PROPHECY (RATIONAL ARGUMENTS COMPARED)

Five argument-families for prophecy's possibility/need/verification:

1. **Need-of-guidance:** R knows God and basic morals but not ritual detail/eschatological measure; wisdom (*hikmah*) + grace (*lutf*) make guidance fitting/necessary → sending is expected. (Mu'tazili/Imami/Zaydi strong form with *lutf*-necessity; Ash'ari/Maturidi weaker "fittingness" form; Athari: need is W-declared, Q 21:107 mercy.) [L6–L15]
2. **Miracle-verification (*dalil al-mu'jizah*):** a prophet-claimant performs an act breaking custom (*kharq al-'adah*) under challenge (*tahaddi*) with inimitability + divine non-countering = truthfulness-certification. Ash'ari flagship (Baqillani *al-Bayan*; Juwayni); Mu'tazili accept with *aslah*-constraints (God must not aid liars — veracity precondition); Maturidi/Imami/Zaydi accept. [L8–L15]
3. **Moral-character:** claimant's perfect veracity/integrity (*sidq/amanah*) corroborates; never standalone (liars can seem virtuous) — auxiliary only, all schools. [L6–L18]
4. **Content-non-contradiction:** message's coherence with R-known truths (unity, justice-per-school) + itsown internal consistency; negative test (contradiction disqualifies) rather than positive proof. [L6–L18]
5. **Testimony-transmission:** mass-transmitted (*mutawatir*) reports of miracles/character deliver certainty (*yaqin*) via K-channel; all Sunni schools + Imami/Zaydi rely on it; its CONDITIONS (numbers, layer-independence, motive-analysis) are hadith-science-adjacent (cf. STEP 105/115). [L9–L15]

School signatures: Sunni kalam = (2)+(5) core, (1)-weak, (3)+(4) auxiliary; Mu'tazili = (1)-strong + (2); Imami = (1)-*lutf*-strong + (2)+(5)-via-Imam; Zaydi = as Imami minus hidden-guide; Ismaili = prophecy reframed as *natiq*-cycle within hierarchy (historical verification subordinated to intellective rank). [L6–L16]

## R. MIRACLES (EPISTEMIC VALIDITY)

Four categories separated: **Mu'jizah** (prophet's challenge-miracle), **Karamah** (saint's non-challenge wonder — affirmed Sunni, denied Mu'tazili-majority as confusion-risk), **Sihr** (sorcery: real-effect vs illusion debated; either way non-evidential because wieldable by liars), **ordinary causality** (customary causation, see S).

Validity conditions for evidential miracles (cross-Sunni consensus): (i) act genuinely breaks custom (*kharq*); (ii) bears challenge (*tahaddi*) + inimitability (*i'jaz*); (iii) occurs at the claimant's hands correlating with the claim; (iv) no counter-performance; (v) transmission reaches *tawatur* (else probative only for eyewitnesses). Qur'anic supreme case: the Qur'an's inimitability (linguistic + content + permanence arguments; cf. STEP 107 linguistic controls — inimitability claims must survive translation-control: the miracle is the ARABIC text, not renderings). [L1/L9–L18]

Can miracles prove prophecy? YES-in-framework (all kalam schools) with the above conditions; NO per Humean-uniformity objection (external philosophical critique, L20 academic: constant-conjunction + testimony-discounting) — answered internally by (a) custom-breakage metaphysics (S) and (b) *tawatur* epistemology (AG). Can natural causation exclude miracles? Only if causation is NECESSARY (philosophers' position, L17 Ghazali debate); if causation is customary (Ash'ari S-position) or contingent-per-wisdom (Maturidi), exclusion fails. The miracle debate thus REDUCES to the causality debate (S). [L11–L18]

## S. CAUSALITY (HABIT, CUSTOM, NECESSITY)

- **Ash'ari occasionalism (L11–L18):** no necessary connection between customary cause/effect; God creates each event (including "effects") directly; "causes" are occasions (*'inda*) within divine custom (*'adah/sunnah* — Q 48:23 *sunnat Allah*); fire does not BURN, God creates burning *at* contact. Ghazali's *Tahafut* Discussion XVII flagship: necessary-causation is unproven; observed regularity (*ittirad*) ≠ logical necessity. Denies NECESSITY, not REGULARITY (AL-Y; AN-E15). [L11–L17]
- **Mu'tazili causation (L6–L8):** real secondary causation: natures (*taba'i'*) and generated effects (*tawallud*) have genuine efficacy; God creates natures, natures produce effects; justice REQUIRES real human causation (else *kasb*-problems). [L6–L8]
- **Maturidi causation (L12–L13):** middle: God creates effects THROUGH (*bi-*) real secondary causes endowed with efficacy; causes are genuine but dependent (*mumkin*); wisdom displays itself IN the causal order rather than around it. [L12–L13]
- **Philosophical causation (L7/L17, falsafa):** necessary emanation (*fayd*) from the Necessary Existent; miracles problematic (Ghazali-Ibn Rushd fault line). [L7–L17]
- **Ordinary human inference (all layers):** all schools affirm practical reliance on custom (medicine, agriculture, craft) — occasionalism never licensed abandoning means (*asbab*); Ghazali explicitly retains *sabab*-reasoning as divinely-instituted custom. [L11–L17]

Vocabulary control: *'adah* (custom), *sunnah* (instituted way), *sabab* (means), *tawallud* (generated effect, Mu'tazili), *fayd* (emanation, falsafa), *iqtiran* (conjunction), *darurah* (necessity). Equivocating "cause" across these = UNSAFE (AH).

## T. ATOMISM (KALAM PHYSICS — HISTORICAL, NOT MODERN)

Classical formulations: indivisible part (*al-juz' alladhi la yatajazza'*); accidents (*a'rad*: color, motion, rest, composition, life, knowledge — inhering one instant, renewed); bodies (*ajsam*) = composites of atoms + accidents; motion/rest as successive accident-creation; space/time as relational (void debated: Baqillani-affirmed *khala'* vs denied); temporal origination of every composite proves a Beginner (bridge to U/V). [L8–L18]

School distribution: **Abu al-Hudhayl/Ash'ari mainstream**: full atomism (attributes-essence-identity in Abu al-Hudhayl pairs with atomism). **Nazzam**: DENIES atoms (infinite divisibility; *kumun*/latency + *tafra*/leap theories). **Baqillani/Juwayni**: atomism systematized as *huduth*-premise machinery. **Maturidi**: atomism accepted broadly (Samarqandi commentaries). **Philosophers**: deny (Aristotelian hylomorphism). **Ghazali**: USES atomist-occasionalist physics against philosophers while signaling its dialectical (not dogmatic) status — physics as *arguable*, doctrine as fixed. **Razi (L18)**: subjects atomism to withering critique, shifting later kalam toward Avicennian contingency (U/V). [L8–L18]

Control: kalam atomism ≠ modern physics (AL-X). No retrojection of quarks/quantum; no apologetic "Qur'an anticipated atomism" via kalam. Historical physics is scaffolding — disposable without doctrinal loss (Razi precedent).

## U. HUDUTH (TEMPORAL ORIGINATION — DISTINCT FROM CONTINGENCY)

*Dalil al-huduth* syllogism (Baqillani/Juwayni form): (P1) every body/composite is originated (*hadith*) — proven via accidents' origination + inseparability (*mulazamah*: no body without accidents); (P2) every originated needs an originator (*muhdith*); (P3) infinite regress of originators impossible; (C) therefore an eternal (*qadim*) Originator exists. Unity-extension: two eternals → mutual hindrance (*tammanu'*, Q 21:22 logic) → one. [L10–L17]

**Huduth ≠ contingency:** *huduth* = began in time (needs a Beginner BECAUSE begun); *imkan* (Avicenna, *Isharat*; absorbed L18) = essence/existence composition (needs a Cause BECAUSE possible-in-itself, even if beginningless). Conflating them merges two proofs with different premises, different vulnerabilities (huduth needs "world began" — physics-dependent; contingency needs essence/existence distinction — metaphysics-dependent), and different critics. AL-W enforces separation. Ghazali's *Tahafut* Discussion I defends world's-beginning against philosophers' eternity; Razi (L18) pivots kalam toward contingency as atomism weakens. [L17–L18]

## V. GOD'S EXISTENCE (SIX ARGUMENTS, FULL STRUCTURE EACH)

**V1. Huduth (temporal origination).** PREMISES: U-syllogism above. INFERENCE: valid modus-ponens chain IF P1–P3 hold. CONCLUSION: eternal Originator. HIDDEN ASSUMPTIONS: accidents-originated (atomism), inseparability, regress-impossibility. CRITICISM: P1 physics-dependent (Nazzam/Razi internal; modern cosmology external — Big-Bang misuse both ways is UNSAFE); P3 needs regress-argument (not assertion). SUNNI EVALUATION: method licit (*nazar* praised, Q 3:191), conclusion affirmed; premises *arguable*, not creedal. [L10–L18]
**V2. Imkan (contingency).** PREMISES: possible-in-itself beings need a preponderator (*murajjih*); regress/circularity impossible. CONCLUSION: Necessary Existent (*wajib al-wujud*). ASSUMPTIONS: essence/existence distinction; PSR-analogue. CRITICISM: distinction itself disputed (is existence superadded?); "necessary" may underdetermine PERSONAL God (needs attribute-arguments K). SUNNI EVAL: accepted as support (Razi onward mainstream), never standalone creed. [L18]
**V3. Design (*'inayah/ikhtira'*; cf. Q 67:3–4, 88:17–20).** PREMISES: ordered contrivance + fine adaptedness observed; contrivance indicates a contriver. CONCLUSION: wise Maker. ASSUMPTIONS: order is objective (vs projection); analogy craft→cosmos licensed. CRITICISM: Humean (order compatible with many hypotheses; analogy-weakness); Darwinian (biological order via selection — affects BIOLOGICAL instances, not cosmic-fine-tuning or metaphysical-design forms; keep scopes separate). SUNNI EVAL: Qur'anic-sign reasoning (*istidlal bi-l-ayat*) affirmed as primary existential path; philosophical over-formalization optional. [L1/L11–L20]
**V4. Fitrah (innate disposition; Q 30:30; hadith "kullu mawlud 'ala al-fitrah").** PREMISES: humans default to transcendence-recognition absent corruption; universality of religion. CONCLUSION: God-known-by-constitution. ASSUMPTIONS: innateness-report reliability; corruption-explanation for atheism. CRITICISM: cognitive-science-of-religion alternatives (agency-detection byproduct); universality overstated (non-theistic traditions). SUNNI EVAL: affirmed as *supportive* (Ibn Taymiyyah flagship, AE), never coercive proof. [L1/L9/L-post-19]
**V5. Moral argument.** PREMISES: objective binding duty + moral order (requital) need a Lawgiver/Requiter. CONCLUSION: moral Lord. ASSUMPTIONS: moral realism (P1) or divine-command metaethics (P2) — the argument's FORM depends on P-outcome. CRITICISM: Euthyphro pressure both ways; naturalist moral realism as alternative. SUNNI EVAL: auxiliary; weight varies by school (stronger for Mu'tazili/Imami/Zaydi/Maturidi-partial; reframed for Ash'ari/Athari). [L6–L18]
**V6. Rational necessity (*darurah*/first-principles; Ibn Taymiyyah's *Dar' Ta'arud* use).** PREMISES: sound reason (*'aql sarih*) + authentic revelation (*naql sahih*) never truly conflict; apparent conflict = misreading of one. CONCLUSION: theistic framework as coherence-condition. ASSUMPTIONS: faculty-reliability; no-defeater status. CRITICISM: begs reliability it assumes (needs AG-epistemology). SUNNI EVAL: methodological maxim affirmed; not an existence-proof per se. [L-post-19]

## W. GREEK PHILOSOPHY (INFLUENCE AUDIT — NO AUTOMATIC VERDICT)

Contact points: Aristotelian logic (syllogism, definition, demonstration — absorbed L7, naturalized by Ghazali *Mi'yar*, weaponized by Razi L18, critiqued by Ibn Taymiyyah *al-Radd 'ala al-Mantiqiyyin*); Neoplatonic emanation (Ismaili cosmology L16; muted in Sunni kalam; Avicenna's synthesis L18-target); Christian-Syriac theology (substance/accident, hypostasis-adjacent vocabulary debates; John of Damascus encounter-tradition); Stoic/atomist parallels for kalam physics (DISPUTED origin).

Two symmetrical errors rejected: (E18) "Greek contact = corruption" (genetic fallacy; AL-AA) and (E19) "rational form = truth" (form/content confusion). Test per thesis: vocabulary-borrowing (ESTABLISHED in cases: *jawhar/'arad*, *mantiq* terms) ≠ premise-borrowing (case-by-case) ≠ conclusion-dependence (rarely shown). Kalam's agenda (sinner, decree, speech, attributes) is demonstrably NON-Greek in origin (F). [L7/L16–L18]

## X. TRANSLATION MOVEMENT (TRANSLATION ≠ ADOPTION)

Abbasid program (al-Mansur→al-Ma'mun→Mutawakkil-era continuity; Hunayn circle; Thabit b. Qurrah; Yahya b. 'Adi later): logic, medicine, astronomy, philosophy rendered into Arabic via Syriac intermediaries. Theological effects: (i) new PROOF-FORMS (syllogistic presentation of huduth); (ii) new VOCABULARY (*jawhar, 'arad, kayf, kam*); (iii) new OPPONENTS (falsafa as rival system prompting kalam self-definition, climaxing Ghazali L17). Non-effects: kalam's QUESTION-LIST predates translations (F); Mu'tazili ethics/eschatology show no Greek parentage; occasionalism/atomism parallels remain DISPUTED (independent-generation hypotheses live). Translation enabled precision; adoption was selective and contested — translation-history ≠ conversion-history. [L7]

## Y. AHL AL-HADITH AND TRADITIONALISM (REASON PRESENT, SPECULATION REFUSED)

## AJ. SUNNI CRITIQUE OF KALAM (STRONGEST OBJECTIONS + STRONGEST REPLIES)
