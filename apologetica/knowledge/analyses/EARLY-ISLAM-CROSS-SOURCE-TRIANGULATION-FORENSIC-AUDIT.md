## G. FIVE-LAYER FINAL HISTORICAL RECONSTRUCTION

1. **Layer 1: What is Established?**
   - Muhammad existed as a historical 7th-century Arabian prophetic figure.
   - He established a monotheistic movement and political community (*Ummah*) in Hijaz (Mecca/Medina).
   - The Qur'an represents the early 7th-century textual baseline of this community.
   - The Hijrah occurred c. 622 CE, establishing the Islamic calendar (*AH* attested by 22 AH in PERF 558).
   - Rapid 7th-century expansion into Levant, Egypt, and Persia (attested by contemporary non-Muslim sources like Thomas the Presbyter and Sebeos).
   - Major state consolidation and epigraphic/monetary standardization occurred under Abd al-Malik (690s CE).

2. **Layer 2: What is Strongly Supported?**
   - The Uthmanic standardization of the consonantal text (*rasm*) in the mid-7th century.
   - The leadership roles of Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali during the early Caliphate.
   - Early administrative continuity utilizing local Byzantine and Sasanian bureaucratic structures before Arabization.

3. **Layer 3: What is Probable?**
   - Specific military engagement frameworks (Badr, Uhud, Khandaq, Hudaybiyyah).
   - Early internal political conflicts (First and Second Fitnas).

4. **Layer 4: What Remains Disputed?**
   - Precise chronological details of pre-Hijrah Meccan activities.
   - Exact numerical figures for battle participants and casualties in early Maghazi accounts.
   - Specific attribution of all variant readings (*qira'at*) to 1st-century Companions.

5. **Layer 5: What Cannot Currently Be Established?**
   - Supernatural/miraculous claims (these transcend empirical historical method).
   - Verbatim accuracy of all speeches and dialogues recorded in late 2nd/3rd-century Sirah compilations.
