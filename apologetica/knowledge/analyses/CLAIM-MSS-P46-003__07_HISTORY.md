# Analysis 07_HISTORY: P46 Physical vs Genealogical Independence
P46 represents a physically distinct early papyrus codex from P66, P75, Codex Sinaiticus, and Codex Vaticanus. This physical independence is established. However, genealogical/textual independence cannot be assumed; early papyri may share Alexandrian text-type affinities, and the actual genealogical relationship between P46 and other stored manuscripts requires full critical apparatus analysis to establish.
