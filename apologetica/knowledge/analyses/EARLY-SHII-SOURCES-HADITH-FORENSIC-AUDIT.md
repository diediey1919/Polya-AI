==================================================
F. AL-KAFI
==================================================

Dedicated forensic audit of Muhammad b. Ya'qub al-Kulayni (d. 329 AH / 941 CE). Composition context in Buyid Baghdad. Structure: al-Usul, al-Furu', al-Rawda. Relies on hundreds of earlier Usul notebooks, evaluated through isnad patterns and regional transmission networks.

==================================================
G. AL-KAFI AND AUTHENTICITY
==================================================

Distinction between canonical importance and historical authenticity. Al-Kafi is not a monolithic witness; individual reports require isolated isnad-cum-matn analysis.

==================================================
H. THE FOUR BOOKS
==================================================

1. al-Kafi (Kulayni)
2. Man La Yahduruhu al-Faqih (Saduq)
3. Tahdhib al-Ahkam (Tusi)
4. al-Istibsar (Tusi).
Compiled 4th/10th to early 5th/11th centuries to harmonize divergent legal traditions; fixed canonical status developed through later scholastic tradition.

==================================================
I. KITAB SULAYM IBN QAYS
==================================================

Complex manuscript history and attribution debates. Contains early Kufan polemical layers mixed with later 8th/9th-century interpolations concerning Saqifa and Alid legitimacy. Neither 100% authentic nor 100% fabricated.

==================================================
J. EARLY WRITTEN SOURCES
==================================================

Investigation of Usul (the 400 foundational notebooks) demonstrates that later Imami collections preserved genuine early written records originating from companions of al-Baqir and al-Sadiq.
