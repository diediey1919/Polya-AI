## 5. HISTORICAL JESUS & PRIMARY SOURCES

- **Historical Core:** Jesus of Nazareth was a Jewish prophet from Galilee, baptized by John the Baptist, who preached the coming Kingdom of God, gathered disciples, engaged in healing/exorcism, enacted symbolic actions in the Jerusalem Temple, and was executed by Roman crucifixion under Pontius Pilate.
- **Source Independence:** Mark serves as the earliest narrative framework; Q (if modeled as independent sayings tradition) and Pauline letters provide early corroboration independent of narrative gospels. Josephus (*Antiquities* 18.3.3 / *Testimonium Flavianum* with interpolated Christian layers) and Tacitus (*Annals* 15.44) provide external Roman attestation.

---

## 6. GOSPEL CHRONOLOGY & SYNOPTIC PROBLEM

- **Relative Chronology:** Pauline Letters (c. 50–60 CE) $\rightarrow$ Mark (c. 65–70 CE) $\rightarrow$ Matthew & Luke (c. 80–90 CE) $\rightarrow$ John (c. 90–100 CE).
- **Synoptic Problem:** Markan priority combined with the double-tradition (Q hypothesis) remains the predominant academic model, though oral tradition and multi-directional redaction models remain active objects of scholarly inquiry.

---

## 7. PAULINE & JOHANNINE CHRISTOLOGY

- **Pauline Christology:** Paul presents Jesus as the Davidic Messiah, exalted *Kyrios* (Lord), and pre-existent agent of creation (Philippians 2:6–11; 1 Corinthians 8:6). While utilizing divine agency categories, Paul's framework operates prior to later metaphysical trinitarian formulations.
- **Johannine Christology:** Opening with the *Logos* hymn (John 1:1), John presents Jesus as the divine *Logos* (*Theos*) incarnate. Greek grammar (*kai theos en ho logos*) emphasizes qualitative divine identity rather than simple numerical identity with the Father, developing high christology into explicit incarnational theology.

---

## 8. PATRISTIC & ECUMENICAL COUNCILS (NICAEA TO CHALCEDON)

- **Nicaea (325 CE):** Addressed Arian subordinationism by declaring the Son *homoousios* (consubstantial) with the Father.
- **Constantinople (381 CE):** Affirmed the full divinity and co-equality of the Holy Spirit against Pneumatomachian opposition.
- **Ephesus (431 CE):** Cyrilline triumph affirming the *Theotokos* and rejecting Nestorian separation of natures.
- **Chalcedon (451 CE):** Defined Christ as one hypostasis in *two natures* (*en duo physeis*), unconfused, unchangeable, indivisible, inseparable, satisfying both Antiochene and Alexandrian concerns (while triggering the Miaphysite/Oriental Orthodox schism).

---

## 9. QUR'ANIC ISA & ISLAMIC EXEGESIS

- **Qur'anic Data:** Isa is designated as *Isa ibn Maryam*, *al-Masih* (Messiah), a *Kalimah* (Word) from God, and a *Ruh* (Spirit) created by His command (QS 4:171), alongside being an honored *rasul* and *nabi*.
- **Rejection of Sonship & Trinity:** The Qur'an explicitly rejects physical divine sonship and trinitarianism as understood in its polemical context (QS 5:116, 4:171).
- **Crucifixion Audit (QS 4:157):** *Wa ma qataluhu wa ma salabuhu wa lakin shubbiha lahum* ("And neither did they kill him nor crucify him, but it was made to appear so to them"). Classical tafsir records various substitution or optical illusion traditions, while modern historical-critical analysis views it as a theological polemic against Jewish/Christian crucifixion claims, and certain modern reformist interpretations suggest apparent execution without physical death.
