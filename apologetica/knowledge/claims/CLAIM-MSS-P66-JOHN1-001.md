# CLAIM RECORD: CLAIM-MSS-P66-JOHN1-001

* **CLAIM_ID:** CLAIM-MSS-P66-JOHN1-001
* **SOURCE_ID:** SRC-MS-003
* **TARGET:** John 1:1 (Gospel of John, Chapter 1, Verse 1)
* **CLAIM_STATEMENT:** Papyrus 66 (𝔓66; P. Bodmer II) preserves the Johannine text of John 1:1 according to the Alexandrian text-type affinities, with a Greek wording consistent with the critical text: In principio ēn ho Logos (Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος). The manuscript begins at John 1:1 and includes subsequent verses through 6:11. The reading aligns with the prevailing Alexandrian textual stream as evidenced by Codex Vaticanus and other Alexandrian witnesses.
* **CLAIM_SEQUENCE:** 3
* **CLAIM_LEVEL:**
  - Primary evidence: Papyrus 66 (original manuscript)
  - Essential comparative data: SBLGNT and Stephanus Editions (via authorized critical apparatuses and digital witnesses) provide overlapping critical editions and modern reference transcriptions; these are secondary, documentary layers but do not constitute supersession of the primary manuscript.
* **SOURCES:** SRC-MS-003; SRC-MSS-001 (Codex Sinaiticus); SRC-MSS-002 (Codex Vaticanus); SRC-CRIT-001 (SBLGNT); SRC-BIB-002 (BibleHub); SRC-BIB-003 (STEP Bible) — where applicable for textual comparison.
* **SUPPORTED_BY:** early second to mid-third-century papyrus witness to John 1:1; consistent with Alexandrian text-type. No substantive deviation from critical text readings is established; many early witnesses (with various manuscript dates) share the same reading of John 1:1.
* **RELEVANT_ADDRESSES/EDITIONS:**
  - P. Bodmer II (Martin & Barns, 1956–1962), published in the Orchid press/imprint context (French-language catalogue). Facsimile and transcriptions available in the Bodmer collection publications and permitted digital collections.

  - The manuscript begins John at 1:1 and covers up to 6:11.
  - John 1:1 reading is the same as other main-line Alexandrian witnesses; nominal independence is not inferred but established by independent physical survival.
