# CLAIM-PAT-IRE-001: Irenaeus Attestation of Johannine Material

## Statement
Irenaeus of Lyons, writing c. 180 CE in *Adversus Haereses* (*Against Heresies*), provides late-second-century literary and historical evidence demonstrating that the Gospel of John, the Johannine Prologue, and explicit Logos theology (referencing framing equivalent to John 1:1) were firmly established, widely circulated, and treated as apostolic Scripture within Christian theological discourse.

## Distinctions
* **Historical Fact:** Irenaeus composed *Adversus Haereses* c. 180 CE and cited/alluded to Johannine texts.
* **Not Autograph Proof:** This patristic attestation is historical evidence of reception and use, not an original New Testament autograph or a primary biblical manuscript witness.
* **Methodological Limit:** Quotations may reflect memory citation, polemical framing, or translation representation (Latin transmission).
