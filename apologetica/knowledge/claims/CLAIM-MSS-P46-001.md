# CLAIM-MSS-P46-001: P46 Primary Manuscript Witness
P46 is a primary manuscript witness to substantial portions of the Pauline corpus, including Romans, Hebrews, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Ephesians, Galatians, Philippians, Colossians, and 1 Thessalonians (with portions surviving in different states of preservation).
