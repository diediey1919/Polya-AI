# CLAIM-HIST-JOSEPH-002: Josephus James Passage (JOSEPHUS-JAMES-001)
Josephus's *Antiquities* XX.9.1 transmitted text references "James, the brother of Jesus who was called Christ" in connection with his trial by the high priest Ananus, providing external historical evidence concerning early figures of the Jesus movement, subject to textual qualification.
