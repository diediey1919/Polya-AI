# CLAIM-HIST-TAC-001: Christian Presence in Rome
Tacitus's *Annals* 15.44 provides non-Christian Roman historical evidence that Christians were present in Rome during the reign of Nero (mid-1st century), and that they were associated with the name "Christus" and subjected to severe punishment following the Great Fire of Rome (64 CE).
