# SANA'A PALIMPSEST

**Common Name:** Sana'a palimpsest / Ṣanʿā' 1
**Collection:** Dār al-Makhṭūṭāt (House of Manuscripts)
**Catalogue Identifier:** DAM 01-27.1
**Repository:** House of Manuscripts, Sana'a, Yemen (primary); additional folios in Eastern Library, Grand Mosque, Sana'a; some in private collections
**Physical Location:** Sana'a, Yemen
**Manuscript Type:** Palimpsest (two layers of text on same parchment)
**Surviving Content:** Approximately 80 folios (roughly half of Quran)

## Palimpsest Structure

### Lower Text (Scriptio Inferior)
- **Script:** Hijazi
- **Content:** Quranic text with significant textual variants
- **Dating:** Radiocarbon 578-669 CE (95% confidence)
- **Characteristics:** No vowel marks, minimal diacritical marks, different surah sequence than standard
- **Critical:** Contains variants similar to companion codices (Ibn Mas'ud, Ubayy ibn Ka'b) but more numerous

### Upper Text (Scriptio Superior)
- **Script:** Hijazi
- **Content:** Quranic text conforming closely to standard Uthmanic tradition
- **Dating:** Late 7th - early 8th century CE
- **Characteristics:** Conforms to standard text with minor orthographic variants

## Discovery
- **Date:** 1972 (during mosque renovation)
- **Location:** Attic of Great Mosque of Sana'a, Yemen
- **Identification:** Identified as palimpsest in 1981

## Radiocarbon Dating
- **Sample:** Parchment from detached leaf (auction lot)
- **Calibrated Range:** 578-669 CE (95% confidence)
- **Date Type:** Parchment date (applies to lower text)
- **Critical Note:** Upper text written later on same parchment

## Textual Variants (Lower Text)
- Surah 2:87: "wa-qaffaynā 'alā āthārihi" (lower) vs "wa-qaffaynā min ba'dihi" (standard)
- Different surah ordering than standard Quran
- Estimated 25× more variants than Ibn Mas'ud codex (Sadeghi & Goudarzi 2012)
- Variants include lexical, morphological, word-order differences
