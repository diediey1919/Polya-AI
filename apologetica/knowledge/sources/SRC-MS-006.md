# BIRMINGHAM QURAN MANUSCRIPT

**Common Name:** Birmingham Quran manuscript
**Collection:** Mingana Collection
**Catalogue Identifier:** Mingana Arabic 1572a
**Repository:** Cadbury Research Library, University of Birmingham, UK
**Physical Location:** Birmingham, England
**Manuscript Type:** Fragment (two bifolios, four leaves)
**Surviving Content:** Surahs 18:17-31 and 19:91-20:40 (partial)
**Script Type:** Hijazi
**Writing Material:** Parchment (goat/sheep)
**Dimensions:** Approximately 32.5 cm × 26.5 cm
**Folios:** 2 bifolios (4 leaves, 8 pages)

## Provenance
- Acquired by Alphonse Mingana in the 1920s-1930s from Middle East
- Part of the Mingana Collection at the University of Birmingham
- Identified and radiocarbon-dated in 2015

## Radiocarbon Dating
- **Laboratory:** University of Oxford (Oxford Radiocarbon Accelerator Unit)
- **Sample:** Parchment
- **Calibrated Range:** 568-645 CE (95.4% confidence)
- **Date Type:** Parchment date (death of animal), NOT writing date
- **Critical Note:** Range includes pre-Muhammad period (Muhammad born c. 570 CE)

## Palaeographic Dating
- **Script:** Hijazi (early Arabic)
- **Scholarly Date:** Late 7th century CE (Déroche, George)
- **Critical Note:** Palaeography suggests later date than radiocarbon range

## Textual Characteristics
- Conforms closely to standard Uthmanic rasm
- Minimal diacritical marks
- No systematic vocalization
- Orthographic variants present but minor

## Scholarly References

- George, Alain. "The rise of Islamic calligraphy." (2010)
- Déroche, François. "Qur'ān of the Umayyads: An Overview." (2014)
