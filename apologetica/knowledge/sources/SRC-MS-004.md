# Papyrus 75 (SRC-MS-004)

## Overview
Papyrus 75 (designated $\mathfrak{P}^{75}$, Bodmer XIV-XV, and Hanna Papyrus 1) is an early papyrus codex containing extensive portions of the Gospels of Luke and John. Paleographically dated to the late 2nd or early 3rd century (c. 175–225 CE), it represents one of the earliest and best-preserved primary manuscript witnesses to the Johannine Prologue and the Lucan text.

## John 1:1 Stored Reading
*Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος, καὶ ὁ λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν θεόν, καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος.* (Scriptio continua in manuscript; verified against critical text apparatus and primary digital facsimile data).
