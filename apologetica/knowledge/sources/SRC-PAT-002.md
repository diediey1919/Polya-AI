# SRC-PAT-002: Athanasius of Alexandria - Festal Letter 39 (367 CE)

## Metadata
* **SOURCE_ID:** `SRC-PAT-002`
* **SOURCE_CATALOG_ID:** `PAT-ATH-39`

## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** Fourth-century ecclesiastical delimitation of the New Testament canon to the exact 27 books currently recognized, formalizing preceding regional development.
* **Cannot Establish:** Original text variants, divine authorship, or universal 1st-century uniformity.
