# SRC-PAT-005: Athanasius of Alexandria — Christological and Anti-Arian Corpus

## Metadata
* **SOURCE_ID:** `SRC-PAT-005`
* **SOURCE_CATALOG_ID:** `PAT-ATHAN-CORP`
* **Author:** Athanasius of Alexandria (c. 296–373 CE)
* **Source Type:** Primary Patristic Theological Source / Corpus (NOT a Biblical Manuscript, Autograph, or Canon List).
* **Languages:** Greek (original composition).
* **Works Included:** De Incarnatione, Orationes contra Arianos I–III, De Decretis, Apologia Contra Arianos, Historia Arianorum, De Synodis, Epistulae ad Serapionem.

## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** Athanasius's theological positions, exegetical methods, anti-Arian arguments, defense of Nicaea 325, soteriological reasoning, pneumatological theology, and ecclesiastical-political narrative.
* **Cannot Establish:** Complete unmediated positions of Athanasius's opponents; first-century Christian theology without historical development; theological or metaphysical truth by virtue of patristic authority.

## Source Dependence Notes
* Athanasius's characterization of "Arians" is polemical and should not be automatically equated with the historical positions of all non-Nicene fourth-century Christians.
* Dating of several works is DISPUTED; the exact chronology of Athanasius's theological development depends partly on work-dating.
* Athanasius's defense of homoousios becomes more explicit and prominent in his later works (De Decretis onward, c. 350s) compared to earlier works.
