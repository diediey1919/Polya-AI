# DOME OF THE ROCK INSCRIPTIONS

**Common Name:** Dome of the Rock inscriptions
**Location:** Haram al-Sharif, Jerusalem
**Type:** Monumental mosaic inscriptions (NOT manuscript)
**Date:** 72 AH / 691-692 CE
**Commissioner:** Umayyad caliph Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan (r. 685-705 CE)
**Script:** Monumental Kufic
**Length:** Approximately 240 meters (inner and outer octagonal arcades)
**Material:** Glass mosaic (gold and colored tesserae)

## Historical Context
- **Construction:** 691-692 CE
- **Political Context:** Second Fitna (680-692 CE); Abd al-Malik consolidating power
- **Religious Context:** Early Islamic identity formation; theological statements to Christians
- **Later Modification:** Caliph al-Ma'mun (r. 813-833) inserted his name in some inscriptions

## Inscriptional Content

### Inner Octagonal Arcade
- **S (South):** Basmala, tawhid (unity of God), Muhammad as servant and messenger
- **SE (Southeast):** Q 33:56 (blessings on Prophet), Q 3:18 (God as witness)
- **SW (Southwest):** Q 3:19 (religion with God is Islam)
- **Contains:** Q 112 (al-Ikhlas) in full
- **Contains:** Q 19:33-35 (Jesus as prophet, not son of God)
- **Contains:** Q 4:171-172 (Jesus as messenger, not divine)

### Outer Octagonal Arcade
- **Contains:** Q 112 (al-Ikhlas) in full
- **Contains:** References to Muhammad as messenger
- **Contains:** Date: 72 AH (692 CE)

### Copper Plaque (Eastern Entrance)
- **Contains:** Q 2:255 (Ayat al-Kursi, partial)
- **Contains:** Q 3:18-19
- **Later Addition:** Al-Ma'mun's name (216 AH / 831 CE)

## Relationship to Quranic Text
- **Verbatim Quotations:** Some passages match standard Quran exactly
- **Textual Variants:** Some passages show minor variations from standard text
- **Significance:** Demonstrates Quranic material in circulation by 692 CE
- **Critical Note:** Inscription ≠ manuscript; monumental inscriptions serve different function

## Scholarly Assessment
- **Earliest Dated Quranic Material:** Yes (as inscribed text)
- **Complete Quran:** No (selective quotations)
- **Textual Stability:** Demonstrates substantial stability with minor variants
- **Function:** Theological/political statement, not scriptural codex

## Critical Distinctions

### Inscription vs Manuscript
- **Inscription:** Monumental, public, theological/political function
- **Manuscript:** Codex, private/liturgical, scriptural function
- **Critical:** Do NOT treat Dome of the Rock as a Quran manuscript

### Date vs Composition
- **Inscription Date:** 692 CE (secure)
- **Quranic Composition Date:** NOT established by inscription alone
- **Critical:** Inscription proves text existed by 692 CE, not when composed

## Independence
- **Physical Independence:** YES (unique monumental inscription)
- **Textual Independence:** NOT APPLICABLE (inscription, not manuscript tradition)
- **Genealogical Relationship:** NOT APPLICABLE
