## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** The fifth-century conciliar formulation defining Christological orthodoxy as one divine-human person (*hypostasis*) subsisting in two distinct natures (*physeis* — divine and human), united "without confusion, without change, without division, without separation."
* **Cannot Establish:** That these exact technical Greek philosophical categories were explicitly codified in 1st-century New Testament texts without subsequent historical and conceptual development.
