## Evidence Boundary & Epistemic Scope
* **Can Establish:** Fourth-century terminological clarification establishing the distinction between *ousia* (essence/nature) and *hypostasis* (individual subsistence/person), summarizing the orthodox Trinitarian formula as *mia ousia, treis hypostaseis* (one essence, three hypostases), and developing advanced pneumatology.
* **Cannot Establish:** That these exact philosophical definitions were explicitly codified in 1st-century New Testament texts without subsequent historical development.
